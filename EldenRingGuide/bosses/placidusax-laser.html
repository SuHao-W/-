DRAGONLORD PLACIDUSAX LASER ATTACK

Short answer: when the lasers start, stop trying to punish. Focus on survival, move sideways, avoid panic rolls, and unlock the camera if lock-on makes the arena too hard to read.

Short Answer

The laser phase is not the time to chase damage. Keep moving, read the beam sweep, and roll only when the hitbox is actually about to cross you. If the lock-on camera hides too much, unlock and track Placidusax manually until the attack ends.

Laser Survival Script

Stop attacking as soon as the laser sequence starts. This phase is a survival check, not a damage window.
Move sideways before rolling. Side movement buys time and helps you read the beam sweep.
Roll late through the beam line. Rolling on the flash often ends too early and gets clipped by the sweep.
Unlock the camera if visibility gets worse. Re-lock only after you survive and can clearly see the boss again.

Reddit-ready short answer

For the laser attack, stop trying to punish and focus only on survival. Keep moving sideways, do not panic roll too early, and unlock the camera if lock-on makes the beam harder to read.

How to Recognize the Laser Attack

Placidusax creates a bright, chaotic arena state before sweeping lasers across large sections of the floor. The danger is not just damage; it is losing your sense of position and rolling too early.

How to Dodge the Lasers

Move Sideways First

Do not stand still and wait. Side movement gives you more time to see where the sweep is going.

Roll Late, Not Early

Early panic rolls often end before the beam reaches you. Wait for the sweep to commit, then dodge through or away from the crossing line.

Do Not Chase Damage

If you survive the laser phase with flasks intact, that is a win. There will be safer punish windows afterward.

Reset Your Position

After the attack, take a second to find the boss and rebuild spacing before attacking again.

Should You Unlock the Camera?

Usually, lock-on is fine for normal attacks. During the laser sequence, unlocking can help if the camera points into brightness, loses the boss, or makes it hard to see the sweep. Try both and use the one that keeps the beam readable.

Common Mistakes

Trying to Punish Mid-Laser

This is the biggest mistake. The attack covers too much space to treat it like a normal recovery window.

Panic Rolling on Flash

React to the beam sweep, not just the bright effect. The visual flash can bait early rolls.

Letting Lock-On Fight You

If lock-on makes the attack less readable, unlock for the sequence and re-lock after you are safe.

FAQ

Can I block the laser attack?

It is usually better to avoid relying on blocking. The sequence is dangerous enough that movement and timing are safer for most builds.

Should I heal during the lasers?

Only heal if you have clear distance. Healing while the beam is actively sweeping often gets punished.

Is this attack easier with light roll?

Light roll can help, but medium roll is workable if you move early and do not panic roll.
