🛡️ Pre-Fight Preparation

Essential Talismans

  • Green Turtle Talisman — faster stamina recovery
  • Great-Jar's Arsenal — wear heavier armor
  • Dragoncrest Greatshield — +20% physical damage negation
  • Bull-Goat's Talisman — +33% poise (prevents stagger)

Recommended Armor

Maximize physical defense and poise. She deals mostly slash damage.

  • Bull-Goat Set — highest poise (100)
  • Lionel's Set — high defense, medium weight
  • Veteran's Set — good balance of defense and weight

Consumables

  • Boiled Crab — +20% physical defense (20%)
  • Exalted Flesh — +20% physical damage
  • Bloodboil Aromatic — +30% damage, -25% defense (high risk)
  • Flask of Wondrous Physick — Stonebarb + Thorny Cracked Tear

Weapon Requirements

  • +10 Special Weapon or +25 Normal Weapon
  • Bleed weapons strongly recommended
  • Jump attacks deal massive poise damage to her

⚔️ Phase 1 — Blade of Miquella

Malenia is fast, aggressive, and has long combos. Her sword has deceptively long range. Never get greedy — one hit at a time.

🗡️ Basic Sword Combos

▼
  • Triple Slash: Right-to-left, left-to-right, thrust. Dodge left on first swing, then roll into her.
  • Jumping Slash: She leaps and comes down with a vertical cut. Roll sideways as she descends — the hitbox is narrow.
  • Spinning Combo: 4-5 spinning slashes. Back away. The final slash has a long recovery — punish it.
  • Running Thrust: She sprints and thrusts. Dodge sideways. Very telegraphed.

💨 Dodge & Counter Patterns

▼
  • Her recovery after combos is longer than it looks. Wait for the sword to fully lower before attacking.
  • She is staggerable after enough poise damage. Jump attacks (R2 while jumping) build poise fastest.
  • When staggered, critical attack immediately — she recovers fast.
  • She can be backstabbed after certain moves (rare opportunity).

🌸 The Flower Bloom (Scarlet Aeonia)

▼

At ~75% HP, she may bloom into a flower. This is NOT Phase 2 — it's a special attack.

  • She becomes a flower and explodes with Scarlet Rot after a few seconds.
  • Run away immediately when she starts the animation.
  • The explosion radius is massive — don't underestimate it.
  • After the explosion, she is vulnerable for 5+ seconds — free damage window.

🌪️ Waterfowl Dance — The Ultimate Test

This is the move that kills 90% of players. When she jumps and hovers in the air, she is about to unleash hell.

⚠️ The Three Flurries

Flurry 1 She dives down spinning — 4-5 rapid slashes. The hitbox extends far forward.

Flurry 2 She pauses briefly, then spins again — slightly shorter range but still deadly.

Flurry 3 Final lunge — one massive AoE slash that covers the entire area in front of her.

✅ How to Survive (Three Methods)

Method 1: The Run-Away Technique (Beginner-Friendly)

▼
  1. When she jumps and hovers — immediately turn and sprint away (hold B/circle + direction).
  2. Keep sprinting for Flurry 1. It should miss entirely if you're far enough.
  3. For Flurry 2 — roll toward her as she starts spinning.
  4. For Flurry 3 — roll sideways or backward at the last moment.

This works best with Light or Medium Load. Heavy Load will get you killed.

Method 2: The Dodge-Through Technique (Advanced)

▼
  1. Flurry 1: Dodge INTO her (toward her) as she descends. The iframes will carry you through.
  2. Flurry 2: Dodge AWAY from her (backward).
  3. Flurry 3: Dodge sideways (left or right) — never backward on the final slash.

Requires Light Load for maximum iframes. Practice on a summoned co-op host first.

Method 3: Shield + Barricade (Tank Method)

▼
  1. Equip a 100% physical block shield with Barricade Shield Ash of War.
  2. When she jumps — immediately cast Barricade Shield.
  3. Hold block for all three flurries. Your stamina will drain but you won't die.
  4. Warning: She heals from blocked hits. This extends the fight significantly.

Only use if you cannot dodge reliably. Bring extra stamina regen items.

🦋 Phase 2 — Goddess of Rot

Triggered at ~50% HP. She explodes into a massive Scarlet Aeonia bloom. Run away immediately when the cutscene ends.

New Phase 2 Attacks

  • Scarlet Aeonia Dive: She flies up and dive-bombs with rot explosion. Dodge sideways as she descends.
  • Rot Clones: Creates 3-4 ghost clones that attack simultaneously. Roll through the real one — clones deal reduced damage.
  • Extended Combos: Phase 1 combos now end with a rot explosion. Back away at the end of every combo.
  • Floating Orbs: Summons rot orbs that track you. Run in a wide circle — they expire after 10 seconds.

Phase 2 Strategy

  • She is slightly slower in Phase 2 but hits harder.
  • Waterfowl Dance still exists and is still deadly.
  • Scarlet Rot buildup from her attacks is dangerous — bring Preserving Boluses.
  • Her flower form after Scarlet Aeonia is a massive damage window — unload everything.

👥 Spirit Ashes & Co-op

Mimic Tear +10 (Best)

Duplicates your entire build. If you use Rivers of Blood, your Mimic will also apply bleed. This effectively doubles your DPS and splits aggro.

Black Knife Tiche +10

High damage, high mobility. She dodges frequently and uses the Black Knife skill that reduces max HP. Excellent if your build is squishy.

Dung Eater Puppet +10

Extremely tanky. Can survive multiple Waterfowl Dances. Use him to draw aggro while you attack from behind.

Co-op Summons

Letich (NPC) is available outside the fog gate. Real players can also be summoned. Note: Co-op increases her HP pool by ~50%.
