🩸 How Bleed Works

Bleed Buildup

Every bleed weapon has a hidden bleed buildup value. Each hit adds to the enemy's bleed meter. When full, the enemy takes percentage-based damage (varies by enemy type).

  • Most enemies: ~15% max HP on proc
  • Bosses: ~7-10% max HP on proc
  • Buildup decays over time if not maintained

Arcane Scaling

Arcane (ARC) directly increases bleed buildup on weapons with Occult or Blood affinity. It also increases discovery (item drop rate).

  • ARC 45 = soft cap for bleed buildup
  • ARC 60 = hard cap (diminishing returns)
  • Some weapons scale bleed with DEX instead (check weapon info)

Frostbite Synergy

Frostbite + Fire is a powerful combo. Frostbite procs, dealing damage and lowering enemy defense. Then hit with fire to reset the frost meter, allowing immediate re-application.

Power-Stancing

Equip two weapons of the same type (e.g., two katanas). Press L1 to attack with both simultaneously. This doubles your bleed buildup rate.

Core Weapons

  • Uchigatana (x2) — power-stance with Blood affinity
  • Nagakiba — longest katana range, excellent for bleed
  • Bloodhound's Fang +5 — still viable, strong Ash of War

🩸 Rivers of Blood Build (Meta)

Equipment

  • Helm: White Mask (+10% damage when bleed procs nearby)
  • Armor: Any medium set — prioritize defense over fashion
  • Talismans: Lord of Blood's Exultation, Rotten Winged Sword Insignia, Millicent's Prosthesis, Shard of Alexander
  • Spirit Ash: Mimic Tear +10 (duplicates your bleed output)

Combat Loop

  1. Apply bleed with L1 power-stance attacks or Corpse Piler (L2)
  2. Bleed procs → triggers White Mask + Lord of Blood's Exultation (+30% total damage)
  3. Continue attacking while buff is active
  4. Use Mimic Tear to double bleed application

💍 Talisman Optimization

Damage Stack (Priority Order)

  1. Lord of Blood's Exultation — +20% damage (bleed proc)
  2. White Mask — +10% damage (bleed proc) [helm, not talisman]
  3. Rotten Winged Sword Insignia — up to +19% damage (successive attacks)
  4. Millicent's Prosthesis — +5 DEX, boosts successive attacks
  5. Shard of Alexander — +15% skill damage (Corpse Piler)

Total potential damage boost: ~+64% when all conditions are met.

Survival Options

  • Dragoncrest Greatshield — +20% physical negation
  • Green Turtle Talisman — faster stamina recovery
  • Great-Jar's Arsenal — +19% equip load (heavier armor)
  • Crimson Amber Medallion +2 — +8% max HP
