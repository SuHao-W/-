FAQ

Should I follow this hub instead of a full walkthrough?

Use this hub when you want spoiler-light direction. A full walkthrough is better if you want every exact item and route.

Is this only for Limgrave?

No. The same loop works in many early and mid-game walls: diagnose, improve, explore, then retest.

Should I grind levels?

Only if natural exploration is not enough. Weapon upgrades, Vigor, flasks, and medium roll usually matter first.
