AFTER RENNALA: WHERE TO GO NEXT

Short answer: level 32 is playable after Rennala, but the next areas feel much safer if you upgrade your weapon, raise Vigor, and explore before rushing the main path.

Short Answer

If you just beat Rennala around level 32, you are not stuck, but you are a little fragile for the next big regions. Before pushing hard into Altus Plateau or Caelid, explore Liurnia, clean up caves and mines, upgrade your main weapon, and aim for more Vigor.

10-Second Plan

If you are level 32 after Rennala

Do not panic and do not rush the next major wall. Spend one short cleanup loop in Liurnia, upgrade your main weapon, push Vigor toward 30, then test Altus or Caelid once regular enemies stop feeling like one-shot threats.

Where to Go Next

Explore Liurnia First

Liurnia still has caves, ruins, churches, Golden Seeds, Sacred Tears, and useful gear. This is usually the safest next step after Raya Lucaria.

Check Mines for Smithing Stones

A stronger weapon often matters more than a few extra levels. If your damage feels low, look for upgrade materials before farming runes.

Try Altus When You Feel Stable

Altus Plateau is a natural next major direction, but it feels much better if you have enough HP to survive mistakes.

Visit Caelid Carefully

Caelid has strong rewards but punishing enemies. It is fine to grab items and leave if the area feels overtuned.

Common Mistakes

Rushing the Main Path

Rennala unlocks respec, but that does not mean you need to sprint into every late-game-looking area immediately.

Ignoring Weapon Level

If fights feel too long, your weapon may be behind. Upgrades usually fix more than random stat points.

Too Little Vigor

Damage stats are tempting, but low HP makes every new enemy feel unfair. Vigor is comfort, not wasted power.

FAQ

Is level 32 too low after Rennala?

It is a bit low for comfort, but not impossible. If you keep getting deleted, upgrade your weapon and raise Vigor before pushing forward.

Should I respec immediately after Rennala?

Only if your current stats are scattered or you changed weapons. If your build works, save Larval Tears until you know what you want.

Should I go to Caelid or Altus first?

Usually, clean up Liurnia first. Then sample Altus or Caelid depending on what feels manageable for your build.
