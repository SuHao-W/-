Tips & Mechanics

Master the Game

Learn essential mechanics and strategies to survive the horrors of Resident Evil 9.

Combat Tips

Perfect Dodge

▼

Timing your dodge correctly can make you invincible for a short period. Watch for enemy attack animations and dodge just before impact to trigger a perfect dodge.

Tip: Perfect dodges can create openings for counter attacks.

Headshots

▼

Headshots deal significantly more damage than body shots. Most standard enemies can be killed with a single headshot from powerful weapons.

Tip: Use precision weapons like the Magnum or Sniper Rifle for maximum headshot damage.

Melee Combat

▼

Conserve ammo by using melee attacks on weakened enemies. Most enemies can be finished with a melee attack after taking significant damage.

Tip: Upgrade your melee skills to increase damage and unlock combos.

Resource Management

Ammo Conservation

▼

Ammo is scarce in Resident Evil 9. Use your weapons wisely:

  • Use weaker weapons on weaker enemies
  • Save powerful ammo for bosses
  • Combine herbs instead of using them individually

Inventory Management

▼

Your inventory space is limited. Manage it carefully:

  • Store items in item boxes when not needed
  • Combine herbs to save space
  • Drop unnecessary items when needed

Exploration Tips

Search Thoroughly

▼

Every room may contain valuable items. Check:

  • Drawers and cabinets
  • Shelves and tables
  • Dead bodies
  • Hidden compartments

Backtracking

▼

Don't be afraid to backtrack. Later in the game, you'll gain access to new abilities that allow you to reach previously inaccessible areas.

Tip: Mark areas on your map that you can't access yet for later exploration.

Advanced Strategies

Enemy Crowd Control

▼

When facing multiple enemies:

  • Use grenades to stun or damage groups
  • Lure enemies one at a time
  • Use environmental hazards to your advantage

Save Strategy

▼

Typewriters are your save points. Use them strategically:

  • Save before tough encounters
  • Save after major progress
  • Keep multiple save files for different paths
