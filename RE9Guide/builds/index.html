Builds & Equipment

Optimize Your Loadout

Choose the right weapons and skills for your playstyle. Below are the most effective builds for different situations.

Recommended Builds

💥 Heavy Firepower (Boss Killer)

Best for taking down tough bosses quickly.

  • Primary: Magnum Revolver - High damage per shot
  • Secondary: Rocket Launcher - Explosive area damage
  • Melee: Combat Knife - Quick finishers
  • Skills: Precision Aim, Quick Reload, Iron Will
  • Equipment: Armor-piercing rounds, First Aid Sprays x5

⚡ Speed Runner (Mobility Focus)

Perfect for speed runs and exploration.

  • Primary: Assault Rifle - Fast fire rate
  • Secondary: SMG - Close quarters combat
  • Melee: Crowbar - Quick and versatile
  • Skills: Dodge Expert, Stealth Master, Fast Heal
  • Equipment: Flash Grenades, Lightweight ammo

🔥 Pyromancer (Elemental Specialist)

Great against plant-based enemies and crowds.

  • Primary: Flamethrower - Area fire damage
  • Secondary: Grenade Launcher - Incendiary rounds
  • Melee: Molotov Cocktails - Quick area damage
  • Skills: Resourceful, Resistance, Quick Reload
  • Equipment: Fire Grenades x10, Gas Mask

🎯 Precision Sniper (Long Range)

Ideal for picking off enemies from afar.

  • Primary: Sniper Rifle - Long range precision
  • Secondary: Handgun - Backup
  • Melee: Sniper Knife - Silent kills
  • Skills: Precision Aim, Stealth Master, Fast Heal
  • Equipment: Sniper ammo x50, Scope upgrade
