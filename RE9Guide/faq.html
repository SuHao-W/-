Frequently Asked Questions

Search FAQs

When is Resident Evil 9 releasing?

▼

Resident Evil 9 is scheduled to release in [TBD]. Stay tuned for official announcements from Capcom.

Which platforms will RE9 be available on?

▼

Resident Evil 9 will be available on PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X/S, and PC. Next-gen consoles will feature enhanced graphics and performance.

Is RE9 a direct sequel to RE8?

▼

While RE9 continues the overall Resident Evil storyline, it features new characters and a fresh setting. It maintains connections to previous games but is accessible to new players.

Will there be multiplayer modes?

▼

Resident Evil 9 focuses on single-player storytelling. However, Capcom has hinted at potential post-launch content including co-op modes.

What difficulty levels are available?

▼

RE9 offers multiple difficulty settings: Casual, Normal, Hard, and Nightmare. Each setting adjusts enemy health, damage, and resource availability.

How long is the main story?

▼

The main story campaign takes approximately 15-20 hours to complete, depending on exploration and difficulty. Completionists can expect 30+ hours.

Are there any returning characters?

▼

Yes, several classic Resident Evil characters make appearances, including Chris Redfield and Jill Valentine, alongside new protagonists.

What is the recommended play order?

▼

While RE9 can be enjoyed standalone, playing Resident Evil 7 and 8 first provides valuable context for the ongoing storyline.

Does RE9 support VR?

▼

VR support is planned as post-launch DLC, similar to Resident Evil 7. The VR mode will offer an immersive first-person experience.

How many endings does RE9 have?

▼

Resident Evil 9 features multiple endings based on player choices throughout the game. The true ending requires completing specific objectives.
