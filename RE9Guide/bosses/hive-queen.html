Hive Queen

Overview

The Hive Queen is a massive insectoid creature that controls swarms of deadly bugs. It can fly and command its minions to attack.

Explosives Flash Grenades Fire

Phase Breakdown

Phase 1 - Swarm Control

▼

Health: 100% - 65%

The Queen stays airborne, commanding her swarm to attack while occasionally swooping down.

  • Swarm Release: Releases insect minions
  • Swoop Attack: Dives at player
  • Acid Spray: Shoots corrosive acid

Phase 2 - Ground Assault

▼

Health: 65% - 30%

The Queen lands and becomes more aggressive, using melee attacks and calling more powerful minions.

  • Claw Slash: Multiple hit combo
  • Stinger: Poisonous attack
  • Call Guardian: Summons elite bugs

Phase 3 - Final Form

▼

Health: 30% - 0%

The Queen mutates, gaining new devastating attacks and increased speed.

  • Explosive Spores: Area damage
  • Mega Sting: One-hit poison
  • Enrage: Increased attack speed

Recommended Build

Area Control

  • Primary Weapon: Assault Rifle (crowd control)
  • Secondary Weapon: Grenade Launcher
  • Equipment: Flash Grenades x5, Explosives
  • Skills: Fast Reload, Resourceful

Weakness Analysis

Key Weak Points

  • Wings: Destroy to ground her
  • Stinger: Critical hit area (3x multiplier)
  • Brood Sac: Explodes when damaged

Tip: Use flash grenades to blind the Queen and her swarm temporarily.
