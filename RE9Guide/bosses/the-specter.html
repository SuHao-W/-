The Specter

Overview

The Specter is a ghostly entity that can phase through walls and attack from unexpected angles. It uses fear and illusions to disorient its prey.

Light Holy Items Silver Bullets

Phase Breakdown

Phase 1 - Manifestation

▼

Health: 100% - 70%

The Specter appears as a translucent figure, attacking from shadows and using basic ghostly abilities.

  • Phantom Strike: Quick melee attack
  • Teleport: Disappears and reappears
  • Fear Aura: Slows player movement

Phase 2 - Illusions

▼

Health: 70% - 35%

The Specter creates illusions to confuse the player while attacking.

  • Clone Creation: Spawns fake copies
  • Mirror Image: Reflects damage briefly
  • Shadow Bind: Traps player in darkness

Phase 3 - True Form

▼

Health: 35% - 0%

The Specter reveals its true form, becoming more powerful and aggressive.

  • Reality Rift: Alters the environment
  • Soul Drain: Drains health over time
  • Final Possession: Instant kill if caught

Recommended Build

Holy Weapons

  • Primary Weapon: Silver Revolver
  • Secondary Weapon: Light Crossbow
  • Equipment: Flashlights, Holy Grenades
  • Skills: Stealth Master, Fast Heal

Weakness Analysis

Key Weak Points

  • Core: Glowing center (3x multiplier)
  • Light Sources: Repelled by bright light
  • Silver: Holy damage effective

Tip: Use flashlights to keep The Specter at bay and reveal its true position.
