Swamp Leviathan

Overview

The Swamp Leviathan is a massive mutated crocodile that inhabits the murky swamps. It uses its size and strength to crush anything in its path.

Fire Grenades Headshots

Phase Breakdown

Phase 1 - Initial Assault

▼

Health: 100% - 70%

The Leviathan will emerge from the water and attack with basic bites and tail swipes. Keep your distance and focus on its head.

  • Charge: Runs straight at the player
  • Tail Swipe: 360-degree attack
  • Bite: Quick snap attack

Phase 2 - Enraged

▼

Health: 70% - 30%

The Leviathan becomes more aggressive, introducing new attacks and increased speed.

  • Water Spray: Shoots pressurized water
  • Death Roll: Grabs and drags player
  • Ground Slam: Creates shockwaves

Phase 3 - Final Fury

▼

Health: 30% - 0%

The Leviathan enters a berserk state, attacking relentlessly with all its power.

  • Mega Bite: Instant kill if caught
  • Whirlpool: Sucks player towards it
  • Finisher: One-hit kill move

Recommended Build

Firepower Focus

  • Primary Weapon: Shotgun (close-range damage)
  • Secondary Weapon: Flamethrower (fire damage)
  • Equipment: Fire Grenades x5
  • Skills: Quick Reload, Precision Aim

Weakness Analysis

Key Weak Points

  • Head: Highest damage multiplier (2x)
  • Eyes: Critical hit area (3x multiplier)
  • Underbelly: Vulnerable when exposed

Elemental Weakness: Fire deals 50% extra damage. Use flamethrower and fire grenades for maximum effect.
