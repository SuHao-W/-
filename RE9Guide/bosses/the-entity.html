The Entity

Overview

The Entity is the source of all evil in Resident Evil 9. This ancient being feeds on fear and despair, manifesting as a formless darkness that can manipulate reality itself.

Light Special Ammo Hope

Phase Breakdown

Phase 1 - Manifestation

▼

Health: 100% - 70%

The Entity begins to take form, attacking with darkness-based abilities.

  • Shadow Tendrils: Grabbing attacks
  • Darkness Wave: Area of effect
  • Illusions: Creates false enemies

Phase 2 - Reality Warp

▼

Health: 70% - 35%

The Entity warps reality, creating hazards and changing the battlefield.

  • Dimension Rift: Teleports player
  • Darkness Orbs: Tracking projectiles
  • Time Dilation: Slows player movement

Phase 3 - True Form

▼

Health: 35% - 0%

The Entity reveals its true form, becoming an overwhelming force of darkness.

  • Oblivion: Instant kill if touched
  • World Ender: Screen-filling attack
  • Despair Aura: Drains health over time

Recommended Build

Light & Hope

  • Primary Weapon: Light Emission Rifle
  • Secondary Weapon: Special Cannon
  • Equipment: Light Grenades x5, Max Healing Items
  • Skills: Iron Will, Fast Heal, Resistance

Weakness Analysis

Key Weak Points

  • Core: Glowing center (3x multiplier)
  • Light Sources: Repelled by concentrated light
  • Special Ammo: Only certain ammo types work

Tip: Use the Light Grenades to create openings and deal massive damage to the core.
