Tyrant Alpha

Overview

The Tyrant Alpha is an enhanced bioweapon created by Umbrella. Its massive size and strength make it one of the most dangerous enemies in the game.

Headshots Heavy Weapons Stun Grenades

Phase Breakdown

Phase 1 - Controlled

▼

Health: 100% - 60%

The Tyrant moves methodically, using powerful but slow attacks. Watch for its tells.

  • Punch: Slow but devastating
  • Charge: Linear dash attack
  • Grab: Attempts to grab and crush

Phase 2 - Unleashed

▼

Health: 60% - 25%

The Tyrant breaks free from its programming and becomes faster and more aggressive.

  • Double Punch: Two consecutive blows
  • Ground Pound: Creates shockwave
  • Roar: Stuns nearby players

Phase 3 - Critical

▼

Health: 25% - 0%

The Tyrant enters a final rage, attacking with everything it has.

  • Frenzy Mode: Increased speed and damage
  • Uppercut: Launches player into air
  • Finishing Blow: Instant kill move

Recommended Build

Heavy Firepower

  • Primary Weapon: Magnum (high damage)
  • Secondary Weapon: Rocket Launcher (heavy damage)
  • Equipment: Stun Grenades x3
  • Skills: Precision Aim, Iron Will

Weakness Analysis

Key Weak Points

  • Head: Critical hit area (2.5x multiplier)
  • Heart: Visible when exposed (3x multiplier)
  • Knees: Stagger on hit

Tip: Use stun grenades to create openings for critical damage.
