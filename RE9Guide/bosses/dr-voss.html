Dr. Voss

Overview

Dr. Voss is the mastermind behind the outbreak. After injecting himself with an experimental virus, he has become a powerful mutated being with access to advanced technology.

Weak Points Stun EMP

Phase Breakdown

Phase 1 - Tactical

▼

Health: 100% - 65%

Dr. Voss uses his scientific knowledge and gadgets to attack from a distance.

  • Energy Blasts: Charged projectile attacks
  • Turret Deployment: Deploys automated defenses
  • Teleport: Uses gadgets to reposition

Phase 2 - Mutated

▼

Health: 65% - 30%

Dr. Voss begins to mutate further, gaining biological attacks.

  • Acid Spit: Corrosive projectile
  • Energy Shield: Blocks incoming damage
  • Minion Summon: Calls B.O.W.s

Phase 3 - Final Form

▼

Health: 30% - 0%

Dr. Voss fully transforms into a powerful bioweapon.

  • Plasma Beam: Continuous energy attack
  • Area Denial: Covers ground with hazards
  • Desperation Attack: Unleashes everything

Recommended Build

Tech Counter

  • Primary Weapon: EMP Rifle
  • Secondary Weapon: Sniper Rifle
  • Equipment: EMP Grenades x3, Stun Grenades
  • Skills: Precision Aim, Quick Reload

Weakness Analysis

Key Weak Points

  • Exposed Brain: Critical hit area (3x multiplier)
  • Energy Core: Disrupt to disable shields
  • Mechanical Parts: Vulnerable to EMP

Tip: Use EMP grenades to disable his shields and tech-based attacks.
