Venomous Flora

Overview

The Venomous Flora is a giant sentient plant that has mutated due to the T-Virus. It uses vines and deadly toxins to trap and kill its prey.

Fire Acid Herbicide

Phase Breakdown

Phase 1 - Root Awakening

▼

Health: 100% - 70%

The plant begins to stir, using vines to attack from the ground.

  • Vine Whip: Long range attack
  • Root Grab: Pulls player closer
  • Spore Cloud: Poison gas

Phase 2 - Full Bloom

▼

Health: 70% - 35%

The plant fully blooms, revealing its deadly flower and increasing attack frequency.

  • Pollen Burst: Area poison damage
  • Venom Spit: Long-range poison projectile
  • Entangle: Traps player with vines

Phase 3 - Seed Release

▼

Health: 35% - 0%

The plant releases its seeds and goes into a final desperate attack.

  • Seed Pods: Spawns mini-plants
  • Acid Rain: Area of effect attack
  • Final Crush: Attempts to smother player

Recommended Build

Fire & Poison Resistance

  • Primary Weapon: Flamethrower
  • Secondary Weapon: Grenade Launcher (incendiary)
  • Equipment: Antidotes x5, Gas Mask
  • Skills: Resistance, Iron Will

Weakness Analysis

Key Weak Points

  • Flower: Critical hit area (3x multiplier)
  • Roots: Destroy to weaken
  • Stem: High damage area

Tip: Focus fire on the central flower for maximum damage. Use fire to prevent regrowth.
