WORLD MAP & PROGRESSION

Region-by-region walkthrough through Lothric. Estus Shards, Bone Shards, and key items.

Recommended Progression Path

Cemetery of Ash → High Wall → Undead Settlement → Road of Sacrifices → Farron Keep → Cathedral of the Deep → Catacombs of Carthus → Irithyll of the Boreal Valley → Irithyll Dungeon → Profaned Capital → Lothric Castle → Grand Archives → Kiln of the First Flame

Optional: Smouldering Lake → Demon Ruins → Archdragon Peak → Consumed King's Garden → Untended Graves → Painted World of Ariandel → The Dreg Heap → The Ringed City

Region Details

High Wall of Lothric (Starting Area)

Boss: Vordt | Recommended Level: 1-20

  • Key Items: Estus Shard (tower), Cell Key (free Greirat), Tower Key (buy from Shrine Handmaid)
  • Notable: Talk to Emma to get the Small Lothric Banner. Free Greirat from the cell below the tower.
  • Bonfires: High Wall of Lothric, Tower on the Wall, Vordt of the Boreal Valley

Undead Settlement

Boss: Curse-Rotted Greatwood | Recommended Level: 15-25

  • Key Items: Estus Shard, Undead Bone Shard, Pyromancy Flame (Cornyx), Mortician's Ashes
  • Notable: Free Irina of Carim from her cell. Find Loretta's Bone for Greirat's quest. Velka's statue for sin absolution.
  • Boss Tip: Attack the white sacs on the tree's body and arms. Don't attack the main body.

Farron Keep

Boss: Abyss Watchers | Recommended Level: 25-35

  • Key Items: Estus Shard, Undead Bone Shard, Golden Scroll, Sage's Coal
  • Notable: Extinguish three flames to open the boss door. Watch out for poison swamp.
  • Hidden: Stray Demon on the bridge (drops Dragon Tooth). Old Wolf of Farron bonfire leads to Archdragon Peak later.

Cathedral of the Deep

Boss: Deacons of the Deep | Recommended Level: 30-40

  • Key Items: Estus Shard, Undead Bone Shard, Small Doll (required for Irithyll), Deep Braille Divine Tome
  • Notable: Talk to Siegward in the well. Free Karla later in Irithyll Dungeon. Patches is here — buy Siegward's armor.
  • Boss Tip: Focus the glowing red deacon. AoE spells and wide-swinging weapons are best.

Catacombs of Carthus

Boss: High Lord Wolnir | Recommended Level: 35-45

  • Key Items: Estus Shard, Undead Bone Shard, Carthus Pyromancy Tome, Grave Warden's Ashes
  • Notable: Break the hanging bridges to access Smouldering Lake. Watch out for rolling skeleton balls.
  • Boss Tip: Attack his glowing bracelets. Destroy all three to kill him instantly.

Irithyll of the Boreal Valley

Boss: Pontiff Sulyvahn | Recommended Level: 45-55

  • Key Items: Estus Shard, Undead Bone Shard, Dorhys' Gnawing, Reversal Ring
  • Notable: Requires Small Doll from Cathedral. Anri's questline continues here. Find Archdeacon McDonnell for Aldrich Faithful covenant.
  • Hidden: After Pontiff, use the invisible path to reach Anor Londo.

Lothric Castle & Grand Archives

Bosses: Dragonslayer Armour, Twin Princes | Recommended Level: 60-80

  • Key Items: Estus Shard, Undead Bone Shard, Grand Archives Key, Crystal Scroll
  • Notable: Kill Emma in High Wall to trigger Dancer early (optional). Three Ascended Winged Knights on the roof for soul farming.
  • Boss Tip (Twin Princes): Lorian teleports — listen for the audio cue. Lothric revives Lorian — kill Lothric while he's down.

Kiln of the First Flame (Endgame)

Boss: Soul of Cinder | Recommended Level: 80-100

  • Notable: Final area. No bonfire until after the boss. Bring all consumables.
  • Tip: Fire resistance helps. Flash Sweat pyromancy + Flame Stoneplate Ring.
