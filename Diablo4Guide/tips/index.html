Tips & Mechanics

Advanced gameplay tips and mechanics explanations

Combat Mechanics

Vulnerable

Vulnerable is one of the most powerful effects in the game. Enemies take 20% increased damage while Vulnerable.

  • Apply Vulnerable through skills
  • Stack with other damage bonuses
  • Excellent for boss burn phases
Stagger

Build up stagger by using Crowd Control (CC) effects. Once staggered, enemies are vulnerable and open to big damage!

  • Stuns, Freezes, Dazes all build stagger
  • World bosses have visible stagger bar
  • Save big cooldowns for staggered targets
Luck Hit

Luck Hit effects have a chance to trigger on hit. Not all hits will trigger them!

  • Each effect shows its chance %
  • Critical strikes don't affect Luck Hit
  • Stack multiple Luck Hit effects
Crowd Control (CC)

CC effects include Stun, Freeze, Daze, Fear, Taunt, Slow, Immobilize, and Knockback.

  • Elites have CC resistance
  • Use Unstoppable to break free
  • Build stagger with CC

Survival Tips

💡 Always

  • Cap your resistances (70% max)
  • Use health potions before you get too low
  • Dodge mechanics - don't stand in bad stuff
  • Learn enemy attack patterns

⚠️ Never

  • Face-tank big attacks
  • Ignore mechanics for extra damage
  • Forget about resource management

Leveling Guide

Early Game (1-25)

  • Complete main story first
  • Pick up side quests along the way
  • Don't worry about perfect gear

Mid Game (25-50)

  • Start building your endgame build
  • Do some dungeons for Aspects
  • Unlock your Paragon board

Endgame (50-100)

  • Push World Tiers
  • Farm Nightmare Dungeons
  • Do Helltides for materials
  • World Bosses when available

Farming Routes

Gold Farming
  • Complete events with gold rewards
  • Sell unwanted items
  • Helltide chests give good gold
  • Nightmare Dungeon end chests
Legendary Farming
  • Nightmare Dungeons - most consistent
  • World Bosses - guaranteed legendary
  • Helltides - target farm with cinders
  • Tree of Whispers rewards
Material Farming
  • Veiled Crystals - salvage rare/legendary items
  • Forgotten Souls - Helltides
  • Fiend Roses - Helltides
  • Coiling Wards - Salvage sacred/ancestral

Paragon System

Paragon boards unlock at level 50. Plan your board carefully!

  • Start with your class's starting board
  • Pick up legendary nodes
  • Connect glyph sockets
  • Level up glyphs with Nightmare Dungeons
  • Match glyphs to attuned colors

💡 Glyph Placement

Count how many nodes match the glyph's color for bonus effects!

Advanced Combat Tips

  • Animation cancel - weave in dodges
  • Group enemies before using AoE
  • Kite dangerous enemies around obstacles
  • Use terrain to your advantage
  • Time cooldowns for damage phases

Social Features

  • Find friends in Clan chat
  • World Bosses need groups
  • Trade with other players
  • Join parties for faster farming
