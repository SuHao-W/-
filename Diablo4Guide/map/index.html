📜 Altars of Lilith

Altars grant permanent account-wide bonuses that apply to all your characters!

Bonus Types

  • +2 to All Stats - Most common
  • +5 to Maximum Life - Health bonus
  • +5 to Maximum Fury/Spirit/etc - Resource bonus
  • +5 Paragon Points - Rare bonus
  • +10% to Experience - Seasonal bonus

Total Collection Rewards

  • All 160 Altars: +160 to stats or paragon
  • Mount: Demon Wings cosmetic
  • Title: "Faithful" or "Touched by Lilith"
  • Puzzle Ring: In-game item

💡 Pro Tip

Use the Map Genie or d4builds.gg interactive maps to find all Altars efficiently. Many are in hard-to-reach places!

👹 World Boss Locations

🌋 Ashava

Location: Fractured Peaks - Near Kyovashad

Spawn: Every 6 hours (check in-game timer)

A massive dragon that spawns in the snowy peaks. Rewards legendary items and mount cosmetics.

💀 Wandering Death

Location: Scosglen - Near Cerrigar

Spawn: Every 6 hours

A colossal undead horror. Requires a full party for the best rewards.

⚔️ Avarice

Location: Dry Steppes - Near Ked Bardu

Spawn: Every 6 hours

A golden-skinned demon. Can drop the "Avarice" mount trophy.

🔥 Mehrunes

Location: Wastes of Hatred - Caldera of Ash

Spawn: Expansion boss, varies

The Lord of Hatred himself. Requires endgame gear and a coordinated group.

⚠️ Spawn Timers

World bosses spawn every ~6 hours. Check your in-game map for the next spawn! Join a Guild or Discord for timer notifications.

🌙 Helltide Events

Helltides are dangerous world events that spawn in random zones. Collect Aberrant Cinders to spend on reward chests!

Event Mechanics

  • 🔥 Duration: 60 minutes
  • 💀 Cinders: Drop from enemies and chests
  • 🎁 Tortured Gifts: Spend cinders here
  • ⚔️ Difficulty: Increased enemy damage
  • 🌍 Zones: Rotates between regions

Tortured Gift Chests

  • Weapon Chest: Swords, Axes, Maces
  • Armor Chest: Chest, Helm, Pants
  • Jewelry Chest: Rings, Amulets
  • Mystery Gift: Any legendary

💡 Farming Strategy

Focus on events (yellow markers) for fastest cinder collection. Save mystery gifts until you have enough for multiple opens!

⚔️ PvP Zone: Fields of Hatred

The Fields of Hatred in Dry Steppes is the dedicated PvP zone. Harvest Crucible Seeds while PvPing!

PvP Mechanics

  • 💀 Open PvP: Attack any player
  • 🌱 Harvest Seeds: From enemies and nodes
  • 📦 Crucible: Deposit seeds for rewards
  • ⚠️ Penalty: Dying drops some seeds
  • 🏆 Ranks: Increase rank for cosmetics

⚠️ Warning

PvP is dangerous! Bring a group for the best experience. Higher ranks have better rewards but face tougher opponents.
