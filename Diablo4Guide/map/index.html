Altars of Lilith

Altars grant permanent account-wide bonuses!

  • +2 to a stat per altar
  • Some grant +5 Paragon points
  • Collect all 160 for maximum bonus

💡 Pro Tip

Collect Altars early! The bonuses apply to all your characters.

Helltide Zones

Helltides are events that spawn in random zones. Collect Aberrant Cinders!

  • 🔥 Helltides last 1 hour
  • 💀 Collect cinders from enemies, chests, events
  • 🎁 Spend cinders at Tortured Gifts of Mystery
  • ⚔️ Enemies hit harder, are more dangerous

💡 Farming Tip

Target farm specific legendary types with the correct Tortured Gift chest!
