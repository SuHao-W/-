The Wandering Death

World Boss

Boss Overview

This massive undead abomination summons hordes of skeletons and uses death magic. Keep the adds under control!

Phase Breakdown

Phase 1: Rising Dead (100% - 70%)

  • Death Wave: Shadow damage wave
  • Summon Skeletons: Many adds - AoE them
  • Bone Spike: Projectile attack

Phase 2: Rotting Away (70% - 40%)

  • Corpse Explosion: Detonate corpses - move!
  • Summon Skeletal Mages: Ranged adds - prioritize
  • Death Grasp: Pulls players - break free

Phase 3: Death Incarnate (40% - 0%)

  • Mass Resurrection: All adds return - AoE fast!
  • Death Nova: Large AoE - get out
  • Final Curse: Debuff - heal through it
