Boss Fight Tips

General Strategies

  • Always learn attack patterns - most bosses have tells before big attacks
  • Bring appropriate resistance gear based on the boss's damage type
  • Save cooldowns for phases where the boss is vulnerable
  • Use the environment - pillars and obstacles can block attacks
  • Don't greed - sometimes it's better to dodge than to get one more hit

⚠️ Don't Forget

World bosses require multiple players. Make sure you're at the right location when they spawn!
