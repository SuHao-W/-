Mehrunes, Lord of Hatred

Final Boss - Lord of Hatred Expansion

Boss Overview

Mehrunes is the ultimate challenge in the Lord of Hatred expansion. This boss features multiple phases, deadly mechanics, and requires perfect execution. Prepare for a long, intense battle.

Phase Breakdown

Phase 1: The Awakening (100% - 75%)

Mehrunes begins with basic attacks while summoning waves of minions. Focus on clearing adds first.

Key Attacks:
  • Hatred Wave: Frontal cone attack - dodge sideways
  • Summon Minions: Spawns Lesser Evils - prioritize killing
  • Fire Breath: Sweeping flame attack
Strategy:

Stay mobile, kill adds quickly, use pillars for cover. Don't stand in front of Mehrunes.

Phase 2: Burning Fury (75% - 50%)

The arena fills with fire, and Mehrunes becomes more aggressive. Watch your positioning!

Key Attacks:
  • Hellfire Pillars: Marked areas erupt with flame - watch the ground!
  • Fury Charge: Fast charge attack - dodge perpendicular
  • Meteor Shower: Multiple meteors fall across arena
Strategy:

Constantly reposition, save mobility cooldowns for charges. The safe zones move - stay alert!

Phase 3: Heart of Hatred (50% - 25%)

Mehrunes reveals his weak point but gains devastating new abilities.

Key Attacks:
  • Detonate Souls: Captured souls explode - free them quickly!
  • Hatred Beam: Tracking laser beam - kite in circles
  • Eruption: Boss slams ground, creating expanding waves
Strategy:

Focus the weak point when exposed. Free prisoners immediately to prevent explosions.

Phase 4: Apocalypse (25% - 0%)

Final phase - everything is thrown at you! This is a DPS check.

Key Attacks:
  • Apocalypse: Massive damage to entire arena - use defensive cooldowns
  • Clone Summon: Creates copies - find the real boss!
  • Black Hole: Pulls you in - dodge away immediately
Strategy:

Burst Mehrunes down. Save all cooldowns for this phase. The real boss has a health bar - clones don't!

Recommended Builds

Excellent for kiting and freezing adds. Great crowd control.

Melee

⚔️ Barbarian - Whirlwind

High mobility and damage. Can survive through mechanics.

Summoner

💀 Necromancer - Bone

Minions distract the boss while you deal damage from safety.

Hybrid

🌪️ Druid - Tornado

Great sustain and consistent AoE damage.

Essential Tips

💡 Do This

  • Bring maximum Fire Resistance gear
  • Use health potions proactively, not reactively
  • Save your ultimate for Phase 4
  • Learn the attack patterns before pushing DPS

⚠️ Don't Do This

  • Don't stand in fire or bad zones for extra damage
  • Don't ignore adds - they will overwhelm you
  • Don't use all cooldowns early - save them!

Rewards

Defeating Mehrunes grants:

  • Unique Lord of Hatred legendary items
  • Exclusive cosmetic items
  • Large amounts of gold and materials
  • Achievement "Hatred's End"
