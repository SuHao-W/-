Belial, Lord of Lies

Story Boss - Act Boss

Boss Overview

Belial is a master of deception. He creates illusions and tricks throughout the fight. Identify the real boss quickly!

Phase Breakdown

Phase 1: Deception (100% - 60%)

  • Illusionary Double: Creates copies - find the real one!
  • Shadow Bolt: Projectile attack
  • Teleport: Jumps around arena

Phase 2: Lies Unveiled (60% - 30%)

  • Mirror Images: More clones, all attack
  • Lie Aura: Random illusions appear
  • Darkness: Arena darkens - watch your step

Phase 3: Truth Revealed (30% - 0%)

  • True Form: Belial reveals himself
  • Massive AoE: Big damage - dodge!
  • Death Mark: Targeted player - spread out!

How to Spot the Real Belial

  • The real Belial has a health bar - clones don't
  • Look for the boss with active abilities
  • The real one always faces a player
