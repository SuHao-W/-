Duriel, Lord of Pain

Story Boss - Act Boss

Boss Overview

Duriel is a massive, ice-based boss with freeze mechanics. The fight takes place in a small arena, so dodging is crucial.

3
Phases
Cold
Damage Type
★★★★★
Difficulty

Phase Breakdown

Phase 1: Frozen Terror (100% - 66%)

  • Ice Slam: Ground attack - jump or dodge back
  • Frost Breath: Frontal cone - don't stand in front
  • Ice Spikes: Spikes emerge - watch ground

Phase 2: Pain's Embrace (66% - 33%)

  • Freeze Wave: Arena-wide attack - find safe spot!
  • Ice Prison: Traps players - break free
  • Charge: Fast charge - dodge sideways

Phase 3: Absolute Zero (33% - 0%)

  • Blizzard: Constant ice damage - keep moving
  • Frozen Ground: Slippery ice - careful!
  • Final Freeze: One-shot mechanic - interrupt!

Essential Tips

⚠️ Critical

Interrupt the Final Freeze at all costs! Use your stuns or CC abilities.

💡 Do This

  • Bring Unstoppable abilities
  • Use fire damage to melt ice
  • Stay at medium range
