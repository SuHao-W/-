Phase Breakdown

Phase 1: Pride and Wrath (100% - 60%)

Azmodan attacks with prideful swipes and wrathful charges.

Key Attacks:
  • Sin Swipe: Large melee combo - block or dodge back
  • Wrath Charge: Targeted charge - move sideways
  • Summon Prideful: Spawns elite demons

Phase 2: Greed and Envy (60% - 30%)

Azmodan creates treasure zones that debuff you if you stand in them.

Key Attacks:
  • Greed Aura: Gold appears - don't stand in it!
  • Envy Bolt: Tracking projectile - kite it
  • Treasure Golem: Spawns a tough golem

Phase 3: Lust, Gluttony, Sloth (30% - 0%)

Final phase with all remaining sins!

Key Attacks:
  • Sloth Aura: Slows everyone - use mobility!
  • Gluttony Wave: Expanding wave of corruption
  • Lust Charm: Confuses players - look away!
