Avarice, the Gold Cursed

World Boss

Boss Overview

Avarice is a treasure-obsessed world boss who creates gold zones that buff him. Destroy the gold piles to weaken him!

3
Phases
Physical
Damage Type
★★★★★
Difficulty

Phase Breakdown

Phase 1: Gold Fever (100% - 70%)

  • Coin Shower: Falling gold - dodge
  • Gold Piles: Spawn gold - destroy them!
  • Treasure Golem: Spawns golem - tank it

Phase 2: Hoard Protector (70% - 40%)

  • Gold Armor: Gains armor - destroy piles to remove
  • Rolling Gold: Boulders roll - dodge sideways
  • Greedy Teleport: Jumps to gold piles

Phase 3: Midas Touch (40% - 0%)

  • Golden Touch: Turns players to gold - help them!
  • Gold Storm: Massive AoE - hide behind pillars
  • Enrage: Super fast - burn boss!

Key Mechanic

Always destroy gold piles when they spawn! They buff Avarice and grant him armor.
