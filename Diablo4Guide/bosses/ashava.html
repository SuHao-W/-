Ashava, the Pestilent

World Boss

Boss Overview

Ashava is a massive world boss that requires a group of players to defeat. This dragon-like demon uses poison and sweeping attacks.

Difficulty

⚠️ Requires Group

This is a world boss - you'll need at least 8-12 players!

Phase Breakdown

Phase 1: Corrupted Scales (100% - 70%)

  • Tail Sweep: Don't stand behind!
  • Poison Breath: Frontal cone - dodge
  • Pestilent Pools: Poison on ground - move

Phase 2: Infestation (70% - 40%)

  • Summon Plague Bearers: Adds spawn - kill them
  • Corruption Wave: Expanding wave - dodge through
  • Poison Nova: Large AoE - get distance

Phase 3: Pestilent Apocalypse (40% - 0%)

  • Ground Slam: Massive damage - far away!
  • Poison Rain: Covers arena - keep moving
  • Enrage: Attack speed increases - burn phase!

Weak Points

Attack Ashava's legs to stagger her. When staggered, the weak point on her head is exposed - attack for massive damage!
