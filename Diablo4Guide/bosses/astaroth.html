Astaroth

Story Boss - Act Boss

Boss Overview

Astaroth is a powerful demon boss with fiery attacks and charging mechanics. This is a relatively straightforward fight but requires good movement.

Phase Breakdown

Phase 1: Infernal Onslaught (100% - 50%)

  • Fire Breath: Frontal cone - dodge sideways
  • Flame Charge: Fast charge - get out of way
  • Summon Imps: Fire imps spawn - kill them

Phase 2: Burning Wrath (50% - 0%)

  • Meteor Swarm: Multiple meteors - watch ground
  • Inferno: Arena fills with fire - find safe spot
  • Double Charge: Two charges in a row!
