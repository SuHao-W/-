Andariel, Maiden of Anguish

Story Boss - Act Boss

Boss Overview

Andariel uses poison and despair to weaken her enemies. The arena will fill with poisonous gas, so positioning is crucial.

2
Phases
Poison
Damage Type
★★★☆☆
Difficulty

Phase Breakdown

Phase 1: Poison Fury (100% - 50%)

  • Poison Spray: Cone attack - dodge backwards
  • Toxic Pool: Leaves poison on ground - move!
  • Spider Summons: Poison spiders spawn - kill them

Phase 2: Maiden's Wrath (50% - 0%)

  • Anguish Wave: Large AoE - get distance
  • Poison Nova: Expanding poison ring - dodge through
  • Despair Aura: Healing reduced - use pots wisely

Essential Tips

💡 Do This

  • Bring Poison Resistance potions
  • Kill spiders immediately
  • Stay at range if possible
