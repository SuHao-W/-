Gearing Tips

💡 Prioritize

  • Main stat first (Strength, Intelligence, etc.)
  • Vulnerable Damage
  • Critical Strike Chance/Damage
  • Cooldown Reduction

⚠️ Don't Ignore

  • Resistances - cap them!
  • Armor - especially for melee
  • Max Life - don't skip
