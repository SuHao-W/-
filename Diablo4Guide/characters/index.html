Classes & Characters

Choose your path - Complete class breakdowns and playstyles

⚔️

Barbarian

Role: Melee DPS, Tank

Playstyle: The Barbarian is a master of melee combat, wielding up to four weapons simultaneously. They excel at close-range combat and can absorb massive amounts of damage.

Key Abilities
  • Whirlwind: Spin rapidly, striking all nearby enemies
  • Hammer of the Ancients: Slam the ground with massive force
  • Rend: Tear enemies apart, causing bleeding damage
  • War Cry: Buff allies and intimidate enemies
  • Charge: Rush forward and knock enemies back
Pros & Cons

Pros: High survivability, great AoE damage, flexible weapon choices

Cons: Melee-only, can struggle with kiting enemies, resource management

Rogue

Role: Melee/Ranged Hybrid, DPS

Playstyle: The Rogue is a versatile combatant who can switch between ranged and melee attacks. They specialize in precision strikes, traps, and shadow magic.

Key Abilities
  • Imbuement: Enchant weapons with elemental effects
  • Shadow Step: Teleport behind enemies
  • Barrage: Fire a spread of arrows
  • Twisting Blades: Throw and recall blades
  • Poison Trap: Lay hidden traps
Pros & Cons

Pros: Versatile playstyle, high burst damage, excellent mobility

Cons: Squishy, requires positioning, complex rotations

🔮

Sorceress

Role: Ranged Caster, AoE DPS

Playstyle: The Sorceress commands the elements, freezing, burning, and shocking enemies from a distance. They are masters of crowd control and elemental destruction.

Key Abilities
  • Fireball: Hurl explosive fire projectiles
  • Blizzard: Summon a frozen storm
  • Lightning Spear: Chain lightning between enemies
  • Teleport: Instantly reposition
  • Meteor: Call down devastating meteors
Pros & Cons

Pros: Best AoE damage, amazing crowd control, teleport mobility

Cons: Very fragile, resource intensive, positioning critical

🐺

Druid

Role: Shapeshifter, Hybrid, Tank/DPS

Playstyle: The Druid can shapeshift between human, werewolf, and werebear forms. They combine nature magic with brutal melee attacks and summon animal companions.

Key Abilities
  • Shapeshift: Transform between forms
  • Ravens: Summon attacking ravens
  • Earthquake: Shatter the ground
  • Maul: Devastating bear attack
  • Tornado: Create a devastating vortex
Pros & Cons

Pros: Extremely versatile, multiple playstyles, great sustain

Cons: Complex gearing, form management, spread out skills

💀

Necromancer

Role: Summoner, Caster, DoT

Playstyle: The Necromancer raises armies of the dead and commands dark magic. They excel at overwhelming enemies with minions and dealing damage over time.

Key Abilities
  • Summon Skeletons: Raise skeletal warriors
  • Blood Surge: Sacrifice health for power
  • Bone Spear: Piercing projectile attack
  • Corpse Explosion: Detonate corpses
  • Decrepify: Weaken enemies
Pros & Cons

Pros: Army of minions, multiple build paths, great DoT damage

Cons: Minion management, corpse dependency, can be slow

Spiritborn

Role: Melee, Speed, Spirit Magic

Playstyle: The Spiritborn channels ancestral spirits to enhance their combat abilities. They are fast, agile fighters who blend physical attacks with spiritual power.

Key Abilities
  • Spirit Strike: Channel spirits into attacks
  • Ancestral Dash: Rapid movement between targets
  • Totem of Power: Place buffing totems
  • Fury of the Ancients: Call upon ancestors for power
  • Spirit Shield: Protective spirit barrier
Pros & Cons

Pros: High mobility, unique mechanics, hybrid damage

Cons: New player learning curve, spirit resource management

Choosing Your Class

Each class in Diablo 4 offers a unique playstyle. Consider these factors when choosing:

  • Melee vs Ranged: Do you prefer to fight up close or from a distance?
  • Solo vs Group: Some classes excel at solo play, others shine in groups
  • Complexity: How complicated do you want your rotation to be?
  • Playstyle Preference: Do you like summoning, spellcasting, or direct combat?
