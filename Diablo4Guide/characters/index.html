Barbarian

Role: DPS Tank

Playstyle: The Barbarian is a master of melee combat, wielding up to four weapons simultaneously. They excel at close-range combat and can absorb massive amounts of damage. Best for players who enjoy aggressive, in-your-face combat.

🔧 Stat Priority

1. Strength (Core Stat) 2. Vulnerable Damage 3. Critical Strike Damage 4. Attack Speed 5. Cooldown Reduction

⚔️ Weapon & Armor Choice

Type Options Best For
Two-Handed Greatsword, Axe, Hammer Max AoE DPS, Whirlwind builds
Dual Wield Sword + Sword, Axe + Axe Attack Speed, resource generation
One-Hand + Shield Sword + Shield, Axe + Shield High tankiness, solo content
Armory System 4 weapon slots Swap weapons for different situations
Core Skills & Rotation

Primary Skills (Generate Fury)

  • Charge: Gap closer + Fury generation
  • Cash In: Spend fury for massive damage
  • Lunging Strike: Mobile single-target

Secondary Skills (Spend Fury)

  • Whirlwind: Main AoE skill - spin to win
  • Hammer of the Ancients: Single target burst
  • Rend: Bleed DoT, great for elites
  • Leap: Mobility + damage

🔄 Standard AoE Rotation

  1. War Cry / Rallying Cry (Buff)
  2. Charge (Gap close + Fury)
  3. Whirlwind (Main DPS)
  4. Ground Stomp (Stun + Fury)
  5. Wrath of the Berserker (Ultimate)
Recommended Gear & Stats

Key Legendary Aspects

AspectTypeEffect
Gohr's Devastating WhirlwindGlovesWhirlwind deals 40-60% more damage while Berserking
Ramaladni's Magnum OpusWeaponSkills that generate Fury reduce Whirlwind CD by 1s
Battle-MadRing+15-25% Attack Speed after killing
EarthquakeBootsGround Stomp creates earthquake cracks

Endgame Stats Target

  • Critical Strike Damage: 300%+
  • Attack Speed: 1.5+ attacks/sec
  • Vulnerable Damage: 40%+
  • Cooldown Reduction: 30%+
Boss Fighting Tips

General: Barbarian excels at sustained DPS but struggles with mobile bosses. Use Charge to follow.

Duriel: Keep moving, use Charge to dodge ice spikes
Andariel: Stack Poison Resist, avoid standing in poison pools
Belial: Save Leap for laser attacks, burst during vulnerability windows
Azmodan: Ground Stomp to interrupt summoning
Pros & Cons

✅ Pros

  • Highest raw DPS potential in game
  • Excellent survivability with proper gearing
  • Great AoE for clearing dungeons
  • Flexible weapon choices
  • Berserker mode for burst windows

❌ Cons

  • Melee-only range - takes more damage
  • Fury management can be tricky
  • Struggles against very mobile bosses
  • Requires good positioning
  • Limited crowd control compared to casters

Rogue

Role: DPS Hybrid

Playstyle: The Rogue is the most versatile class, switching seamlessly between melee and ranged combat. They excel at burst damage, trap placement, and shadow magic. Perfect for players who enjoy combo-based rotations and high mobility.

🔧 Stat Priority

1. Dexterity (Core Stat) 2. Critical Strike Chance 3. Critical Strike Damage 4. Trap Damage 5. Shadow Damage

⚔️ Weapon & Armor Choice

Type Options Best For
Dual Daggers Dagger + Dagger Melee Twisting Blades builds
Bow + Dagger Ranged + Melee Rapid Fire / Barrage builds
Crossbow + Dagger Heavy hits + Mobility Penetrating Shot builds
Light Armor Focus on evade All builds - Rogue has innate dodge
Core Skills & Rotation

Specialization Choices

  • Combo: Basic skills generate Combo Points, spend for massive damage
  • Trap: Lay陷阱 before fights, increased trap damage
  • Shadow: More shadow skills, shadow clone imitation

Key Skills by Build

  • Twisting Blades: Throw blades, recall for explosion
  • Rapid Fire: Ranged single-target DPS
  • Penetrating Shot: Pierce through groups
  • Shuriken: Cheap ranged damage

🔄 Twisting Blades Rotation

  1. Shadow Step (Gap close)
  2. Twisting Blades (Apply)
  3. Spam Basic until 4 Combo Points
  4. Dash (Generate Combo)
  5. Twisting Blades (Spend Combo)
  6. Smoke Grenade (Survivability)

🔄 Rapid Fire Rotation

  1. Caltrops (Zone control)
  2. Poison Trap (DoT)
  3. Rapid Fire (Main DPS)
  4. Frontal Turret (Burst window)
  5. Shadow Step (Reposition)
Recommended Gear & Stats

Key Legendary Aspects

AspectTypeEffect
Open RogueGlovesFirst attack from stealth guarantees crit
UmbraticChestShadow clone lasts 6s, deals 30% damage
Cheat DeathBoots70% damage reduction when below 20% HP
UnstableRingCrits cause explosion

Imbue Types

  • Cold: Freeze enemies, control
  • Poison: DoT, healing reduction
  • Shadow: Extra damage + slow
  • Lightning: Chain damage
Boss Fighting Tips

General: Rogues excel at single-target boss damage with Rapid Fire. Use traps and positioning carefully.

Duriel: Stay ranged, save Shadow Step for Charge attacks
Andariel: Use Cold Imbue to freeze self, avoid poison
Belial: Burst during phase transitions, save mobility
Azmodan: Trap placement before laser phase
Pros & Cons

✅ Pros

  • Best single-target burst damage
  • Excellent mobility with Shadow Step
  • Can play melee or ranged
  • Great trap utilities
  • High skill expression ceiling

❌ Cons

  • Squishy - low survivability
  • Complex combo system
  • Resource management (Energy) is tight
  • Requires excellent positioning
  • Traps can be tricky to use

Sorceress

Role: DPS Control

Playstyle: The Sorceress commands the elements, unleashing devastating AoE damage and unparalleled crowd control. They can freeze, burn, and shock enemies from safety. Best for players who enjoy strategic positioning and spell combos.

🔧 Stat Priority

1. Intelligence (Core Stat) 2. Critical Strike Chance 3. Cooldown Reduction 4. Fire/Cold/Lightning Damage 5. Mana Regeneration

⚔️ Weapon & Armor Choice

Type Options Best For
Focus + Source Orb + Tome Maximized spell damage
Wand + Shield Magic + Defense Tankier builds
Staff All-in-one Meteor / Fireball builds
Light Armor Focus on mana All builds - spell efficiency
Core Skills & Rotation

Elemental Specialties

  • Fire: Highest raw damage, burning DoT
  • Cold: Freeze control, attack speed slow
  • Lightning: Chain damage, mana generation
  • Mixed: Combine elements for bonuses

Key Skills by Build

  • Meteor: Massive AoE burst, requires setup
  • Blizzard: Sustained AoE damage + slow
  • Frozen Orb: Balanced AoE + freeze
  • Chain Lightning: Group clear, mana efficient

🔄 Fire Ball / Meteor Rotation

  1. Hydra (持久 damage)
  2. Fireball (Build to Meteor)
  3. Flame Shield (Defense)
  4. Meteor (Main burst)
  5. Inferno (Close-range AoE)

🔄 Blizzard / Frost Rotation

  1. Frost Nova (Freeze nearby)
  2. Blizzard (Sustained damage)
  3. Ice Spike (Single target)
  4. Frozen Orb (Area freeze)
  5. Teleport (Reposition)
Recommended Gear & Stats

Key Legendary Aspects

AspectTypeEffect
Meteor's LegacyStaffMeteor falls twice
ProdigyGlovesCasting Basic generates Mana
UndestructibleChestAuto-revive once per 90s
ConflagrateOffhandFireball leaves exploding trail

Endgame Stats Target

  • Mana: 100+
  • Cooldown Reduction: 40%+ (for perma-CC)
  • Critical Strike Chance: 30%+
  • Attack Speed: 1.0+ (for mana regen)
Boss Fighting Tips

General: Sorceress is the squishiest class but has best crowd control. Survival depends on positioning and Teleport usage.

Duriel: Constant Blizzard, keep Teleport for ice prisons
Andariel: Fire Shield + Flame Wall, kite poison
Belial: Freeze Nova to interrupt summoning phase
Azmodan: Lightning Chain for minions, Meteor for laser
Pros & Cons

✅ Pros

  • Best AoE damage in game
  • Excellent crowd control (freeze, stun)
  • Great mobility with Teleport
  • Safe ranged gameplay
  • Synergistic elemental combos

❌ Cons

  • Most fragile class
  • Mana management is challenging
  • Long cooldowns on key spells
  • Requires constant repositioning
  • Vulnerable to interrupt effects

Druid

Role: Hybrid Tank

Playstyle: The Druid is the ultimate shapeshifter, transforming between human, werewolf, and werebear forms. They combine nature magic with brutal melee attacks and summoning. Best for players who enjoy versatility and transformational combat.

🔧 Stat Priority

1. Willpower (Core Stat) 2. Critical Strike Damage 3. Attack Speed 4. Companion Damage 5. Shapeshift Damage

⚔️ Weapon & Armor Choice

Type Options Best For
Two-Handed Staff, Spearo Spells + Nature Magic
Dual Wield Claws, Daggers Shapeshift builds
One-Hand + Offhand Mace + Totem Summoner builds
Heavy Armor Fortify builds Tank / Werebear
Core Skills & Rotation

Form System

  • Human: Access to all skills, spells
  • Werewolf: Fast attacks, Bleed, mobility
  • Werebear: Heavy hits, Fortify, tankiness

Key Skills by Build

  • Tornado: Persistent AoE, Hurricane synergy
  • Werewolf Maul: Fast single-target
  • Trample: Werebear charge
  • Pet Druid: Ravens, Wolves, Vines

🔄 Tornado / Hurricane Rotation

  1. Hurricane (Channel AoE)
  2. Tornado (Procs from Hurricane)
  3. Wilds (Summon plants)
  4. Debilitating Roar (Defensive)
  5. Earth Spike (Interrupt)

🔄 Werewolf / Shred Rotation

  1. Debilitating Roar (BUFF)
  2. Werewolf (Transform)
  3. Shred (Main damage)
  4. Blood Howl (Heal + Fury)
  5. Rabies (Poison DoT)
Recommended Gear & Stats

Key Legendary Aspects

AspectTypeEffect
SkinwalkersArmorGain Fortify when shapeshifted
StormclawClawsWind shear deals 40% more
Mighty StormOffhandTornadoes merge together
InsentientRingSummons deal 40% of your damage

Companion Skills

  • Ravens: Swoop attack, +4 canisters
  • Wolves: Melee pets, Frost Wolf for slow
  • Vines: Root enemies, Nature's Reach
Boss Fighting Tips

General: Druid is tanky but has slow damage. Use Werebear form for dangerous phases, Werewolf for DPS windows.

Duriel: Stay Werebear, Hurricane for ice phases
Andariel: Werewolf with Poison Resist, Blood Howl sustain
Belial: Burst during human phase, CC during demon form
Azmodan: Pet distraction + Tornado damage
Pros & Cons

✅ Pros

  • Most versatile class - spells + melee + pets
  • Great survivability in Werebear form
  • Excellent DoT with Rabies + Tornado
  • Strong pet synergies
  • Good solo or group play

❌ Cons

  • Slow attack speed
  • Complex form management
  • Spread thin - less specialization
  • Pet AI can be unreliable
  • Resource (Spirit) is tight

Necromancer

Role: DPS Summoner

Playstyle: The Necromancer commands an army of the undead and wields powerful dark magic. They excel at overwhelming enemies with minions and dealing damage over time. Best for players who enjoy pet armies and strategic curse usage.

🔧 Stat Priority

1. Intelligence (Core Stat) 2. Minion Damage 3. Critical Strike Damage 4. Damage over Time 5. Cooldown Reduction

⚔️ Weapon & Armor Choice

Type Options Best For
Two-Handed Scythe, Sword Bone Spear / Corpse builds
One-Hand + Shield Sword + Shield Tankier minion builds
Focus + Source Orb + Tome Spell-focused builds
Armor Priority Minion damage Pet builds - let minions deal damage
Core Skills & Rotation

Book of the Dead - Minion System

  • Skeleton Warriors: Frontline meat shields
  • Skeleton Mages: Ranged damage (can be Cold/Fire/Shadow)
  • Golem: Tanky tank - draw aggro
  • Blood Surge: Sacrifice minions for power

Key Skills by Build

  • Bone Spear: Single target pierce
  • Corpse Explosion: AoE from corpses
  • Blood Surge: AoE damage + shield
  • Reap: Basic skill, generates corpses

🔄 Bone Spear Rotation

  1. Decrepify (Curse enemies)
  2. Bone Spear (Main damage)
  3. Corpse Explosion (Clean up)
  4. Blood Surge (Shield + damage)
  5. Bone Prison (Trap boss)

🔄 Minion Master Rotation

  1. Summon Skeletons (Army)
  2. Raise Golem (Tank)
  3. Corpse Explosion (Spread)
  4. Blood Wave (Heal minions)
  5. Decrepify (Debuff)
Recommended Gear & Stats

Key Legendary Aspects

AspectTypeEffect
OsseoHelmMinions gain +50% attack speed
Blood GetterGlovesBlood Surge generates more essence
ExplosiveChestCorpse Explosion has 2 charges
PlagueWeaponBlight slows + increases DoT

Unique Items

  • Ring of the Midnight: Minions explode on death
  • Blood Salt: Blood Surge heals more
  • Corpse Tender: Corpse skills have no cooldown
Boss Fighting Tips

General: Necromancer is tanky due to shields and pets. Golem tanks boss attention while you cast from behind.

Duriel: Golem tanks ice spikes, Bone Spear from range
Andariel: Poison minions absorb damage, Iron Maiden
Belial: Bone Prison locks boss, maximize DPS
Azmodan: Army distracts minions, focus boss
Pros & Cons

✅ Pros

  • Strong pet army for aggro management
  • Great DoT with Blight + corpse explosions
  • Excellent survivability with shields
  • Good crowd control with curses
  • Flexible - pets, spells, or hybrid

❌ Cons

  • Pet AI can be frustrating
  • Depends on corpses for some skills
  • Minions can die in hard content
  • Bone Spear is single target
  • Essence management is tricky

Spiritborn

Role: DPS Hybrid

Playstyle: The Spiritborn channels ancestral spirits to enhance their combat abilities. They are fast, agile fighters who blend physical attacks with spiritual power. Best for players who enjoy kinetic, combo-based combat with spirit synergies.

🔧 Stat Priority

1. Dexterity (Core Stat) 2. Spirit Damage 3. Critical Strike Damage 4. Attack Speed 5. Milli秒

⚔️ Weapon & Armor Choice

Type Options Best For
Quarterstaff Two-handed Maximum spirit damage
Dual Wield Centennial Attack speed builds
Two-Handed Glaive Ranged / Throw builds
Armor Light Dodge-based survivability
Core Skills & Spirit System

Spirit Guardians

  • Eagle: Lightning, mobility, attack speed
  • Wolf: Poison, bleed, attack while moving
  • Jaguar: Melee, criticals, evasion
  • Centipede: Defensive, barrier, regeneration

Key Skills by Build

  • Quill Shot: Primary, spirit generation
  • Razor Wings: Spinning attack
  • Devouring Swarm: Melee dot
  • Rock Splitter: Heavy single target

🔄 Eagle / Jaguar Hybrid Rotation

  1. Eagle Spirit (Primary)
  2. Quill Shot (Spirit gen)
  3. Razor Wings (DPS)
  4. Swoop (Gap close)
  5. Jaguar Shift (Burst)
Recommended Gear & Stats

Key Legendary Aspects

EclecticGlovesSwap spirits for damage bonus
ProdigyBootsSkills cost less spirit
RacialWeaponCentipede deals poison

Endgame Stats Target

  • Spirit Cost Reduction: 30%+
  • Attack Speed: 1.8+
  • Dodge Chance: 20%+
Boss Fighting Tips

General: Spiritborn is fastest class with best mobility. Use Eagle for speed, Jaguar for burst, Centipede for defense.

Duriel: Eagle spirit for dodging, keep moving
Andariel: Centipede for defense, manage毒 spread
Belial: Jaguar for burst phases
Azmodan: Wolf for continuous damage
Pros & Cons

✅ Pros

  • Fastest class in game
  • Excellent mobility and evasion
  • High skill expression
  • Flexible spirit combinations
  • Great single target damage

❌ Cons

  • New class - less established builds
  • Squishy without Centipede
  • Complex spirit management
  • Relies on dodging
  • Requires good timing
