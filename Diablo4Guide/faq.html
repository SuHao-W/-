❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Diablo 4: Lord of Hatred

🎮 Game Basics

What is Diablo 4: Lord of Hatred?

Lord of Hatred is the first major expansion for Diablo 4, featuring:

  • 🔥 New class: Spiritborn
  • 🗺️ New zone: The Wastes of Hatred
  • ⚔️ New endgame: The Pit
  • 📜 New storyline focusing on demon lord Mehrunes
Do I need the base game for the expansion?

Yes! Lord of Hatred is an expansion that requires the base Diablo 4 game. You cannot play the expansion without owning Diablo 4 first.

What level should I be for expansion content?

We recommend:

  • 🎯 Level 50+ with decent gear for normal expansion content
  • ⚔️ Level 60+ with good gear for The Pit
  • 👹 Level 70+ with optimized build for endgame activities
Is progress from base game kept in expansion?

Yes! Your characters, paragon, inventory, and unlocked content carry over. Only the new expansion-specific content is added.

👤 Characters & Classes

What is the Spiritborn class?

Spiritborn is a melee class that channels ancestral spirits:

  • ⚡ Uses Spirit as primary resource
  • 🛡️ Places totems to buff/debuff
  • 🌟 Has Quill, Grizzly, Panther, and Eagle spirit powers
  • 💨 High mobility and burst damage
Which class is best for beginners?

Recommended for beginners:

  • 🗡️ Rogue - Ranged options, good mobility, moderate difficulty
  • 🪓 Barbarian - Tanky, straightforward, high survivability
  • ✨ Spiritborn - New player-friendly, strong early and late

Challenging classes: Necromancer (complex minion management), Sorcerer (squishy early game)

Can I respec my skills and paragon?

Yes! Both can be respecced:

  • 🎯 Skills: Visit Class Trainer in any city. Cost scales with level.
  • 📊 Paragon: Free to respec at any time!
  • ⚡ Skill Points: Return to any Class Trainer
How many characters can I have?

You can create one character per class, for a total of 6 characters (5 base + Spiritborn). Each can be customized with different builds!

⚔️ Gameplay & Mechanics

What are World Tiers?

World Tiers are difficulty settings affecting enemy strength, XP, and loot:

  • ⚪ WT1 Normal: Story mode, casual play
  • 🔵 WT2 Veteran: Slight challenge, better loot
  • 🟡 WT3 Nightmare: Challenging, sacred items drop
  • 🟠 WT4 Torment: Hardest, ancestral items, best loot
How do I unlock higher World Tiers?

Complete capstone dungeons:

  • 🏆 WT3: Complete Cathedral of Light capstone (recommended level 50+)
  • 👹 WT4: Complete Fallen Temple capstone (recommended level 60+)

💡 Tip

Grinding WT3 until you have good gear makes WT4 much easier!

What are Aspects and how do they work?

Aspects are legendary powers you can transfer between items:

  1. 📦 Find a legendary item with a good aspect
  2. 🔬 Extract at the Occultist (costs material)
  3. ✨ Imprint onto rare/magic item

Codex: Run specific dungeons to unlock aspects permanently in your Codex!

What is Overpower damage?

Overpower is bonus damage based on your HP and Fortify:

  • 💚 Triggered by dealing damage while at high HP
  • ⚔️ Damage scales with (Current HP + Fortify) × Overpower %
  • 🔥 Works with critical hits
  • 🪓 Best for Barbarian and Druid builds
What does Vulnerable mean?

Vulnerable status causes enemies to take 20% increased damage:

  • ❄️ Applied by specific skills and aspects
  • ⏱️ Lasts until the enemy is damaged
  • 💥 Excellent for burst damage windows
How does the seasonal system work?

Each season brings new content and mechanics:

  • 📅 Duration: ~3-4 months per season
  • 🎮 Fresh start: New seasonal character (can still access eternal)
  • 🏆 Seasonal Journey: Complete challenges for rewards
  • 💎 Seasonal blessings: Powerful temporary buffs

🏆 Endgame

What is The Pit?

The Pit is an endgame activity in the expansion:

  • ⛏️ Fight waves of enemies in timed runs
  • 💎 Earn Obducite to craft gem upgrades
  • 📈 Higher pits = better gem ranks
  • 🏆 Push leaderboards
What should I do at level 100?

Endgame activities:

  • 🏰 Push Nightmare Dungeon tiers
  • 🔥 Farm Helltides for legendaries
  • 👹 Battle World Bosses
  • 📜 Complete Seasonal Journey
  • ⚔️ Try different builds
  • 🏆 Achieve Level 100 Paragon boards
How do I get the best gear?

Progression for best loot:

  1. 🎯 Complete seasonal journey for guaranteed BIS items
  2. 👹 Farm WT4 World Bosses
  3. 🔥 Run Helltide events
  4. 🏰 Push Nightmare Dungeon tiers
  5. 💎 The Pit for gem upgrades
What are Nightmare Dungeons?

Nightmare Dungeons are harder versions of regular dungeons:

  • 📈 Affixes increase difficulty (e.g., +elite damage)
  • 💎 Level up your Glyphs here
  • 📦 Better loot than regular dungeons
  • 🔑 Require Sigils to enter
What are Altars of Lilith?

160 collectible Altars across Sanctuary:

  • ✨ Grant permanent account-wide bonuses
  • 📊 +2 All Stats, +5 Max Life, +5 Paragon Points
  • 🏆 Complete all for mount and title rewards

👥 Multiplayer & Social

Can I play with friends?

Yes! Diablo 4 supports full co-op:

  • 👥 Parties up to 4 players
  • 🎮 Play entire campaign together
  • ⚔️ All content accessible in groups
  • 📊 Difficulty scales with party size
How does trading work?

Trading has restrictions:

  • 🤝 Trade with party members only
  • ⏰ Items must be recently dropped (time window)
  • ❌ Cannot trade quest items orbind-on-equip
  • 💰 Gold trading unlimited between players
What are Clans?

Clans are player communities:

  • 💬 Shared clan chat
  • 👥 Up to 150 members
  • 🤝 Help coordinate activities
  • 🏆 Clan achievements

⚙️ Technical & Support

Is Diablo 4 always online?

Yes! Diablo 4 requires constant internet connection:

  • 🌐 Always-online to prevent cheating
  • 📡 Single-player still requires connection
  • ⚠️ No offline mode available
Controller support?

Full controller support on PC:

  • 🎮 Native console-like experience
  • 🔄 Seamless keyboard/mouse to controller switching
  • 📱 Also available on console
Cross-play and Cross-progression?

Yes to both!

  • 🎯 Cross-play: Play with friends on any platform
  • 💾 Cross-progression: Same Battle.net account progresses across platforms
  • ⚠️ Different platforms = separate progression
How to report bugs or feedback?

Report through these channels:

  • 🎮 In-game Feedback menu (Esc → Feedback)
  • 🌐 Official Blizzard forums
  • 🐛 Bug Report section on forums
