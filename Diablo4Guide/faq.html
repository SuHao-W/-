Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions and answers about Diablo 4: Lord of Hatred

Game Basics

What is Diablo 4: Lord of Hatred?

Lord of Hatred is the first major expansion for Diablo 4, featuring a new class (Spiritborn), a new zone, new endgame content, and a new storyline focusing on the demon Mehrunes.

Do I need to buy the expansion separately?

Yes, Lord of Hatred is a paid expansion. However, you need the base game of Diablo 4 to play it.

What level should I be for expansion content?

It's recommended to be at least level 50 and have finished the base game campaign before starting the expansion content.

Characters & Classes

What is the Spiritborn class?

Spiritborn is a new melee class that channels ancestral spirits. They use unique spirit mechanics, have high mobility, and can place totems to buff themselves and allies.

Which class is best for beginners?

Rogue or Barbarian are great for beginners. Rogue has ranged options and good mobility, while Barbarian is tanky and straightforward to play.

Can I respec my skills?

Yes! You can respec your skills at any time by visiting a Class Trainer in any major city. Respec costs scale with your level but remain affordable.

Gameplay & Mechanics

What is World Tier?

World Tiers are difficulty settings that affect enemy strength, experience gained, and loot quality. Higher tiers offer better rewards.

  • World Tier 1 - Normal
  • World Tier 2 - Veteran
  • World Tier 3 - Nightmare
  • World Tier 4 - Torment
How do I unlock higher World Tiers?

Complete the capstone dungeons for each tier:

  • Tier 3: Complete Cathedral of Light Capstone
  • Tier 4: Complete Fallen Temple Capstone
What are Aspects?

Aspects are legendary powers that you can extract from legendary items and imprint onto other items. They let you customize your build!

How does the Paragon system work?

Paragon unlocks at level 50. You get points for each level beyond 50 to spend on Paragon boards. Each board has nodes, glyph sockets, and legendary nodes.

What is a Nightmare Dungeon?

Nightmare Dungeons are modified, more difficult versions of regular dungeons with affixes. They level up glyphs and give good loot!

Endgame

What should I do at level 100?

At level 100, you can push higher Nightmare Dungeon tiers, collect perfect gear, complete achievements, try new builds, or help friends!

How often do World Bosses spawn?

World Bosses spawn approximately every 6 hours. Check your in-game map for timers and locations!

What are Helltides?

Helltides are hour-long events in random zones where enemies are tougher, but you can collect Aberrant Cinders to open special chests.

Multiplayer & Social

Can I play with friends?

Yes! Diablo 4 supports co-op parties of up to 4 players. You can play through the entire campaign together.

Is there trading?

Yes, but with restrictions. You can trade certain items with party members within a time window, or use the trade channels in towns.

What are clans?

Clans are groups of players you can join or create. Clans have shared chat and can help coordinate group activities.

Technical

Is Diablo 4 always online?

Yes, Diablo 4 requires an internet connection at all times, even for solo play.

Can I play with controller?

Yes! Diablo 4 has full controller support on PC, and is designed to play well with controller.

How do I report bugs?

You can report bugs through the in-game feedback system or on the official Blizzard forums.
