🎪 Festival Setup

Radahn's fight is unique — it's a festival battle where you can summon NPC allies. Do NOT attempt this fight solo unless you're severely overleveled.

Available Summons (Gold Signs on the Battlefield)

  • Blaidd the Half-Wolf — aggressive melee, high damage
  • Alexander (Iron Fist) — tanky, draws aggro
  • Okina — fast bleed attacks
  • Patches — cowardly, runs away after one hit (but distracts Radahn)
  • Lionel the Lionhearted — heavy tank

Important: Signs reappear after NPCs die. Keep re-summoning throughout the fight.

Pre-Fight Checklist

  • ✅ Summon Torrent (horse) — essential for this fight
  • ✅ Equip a bow or ranged option for Phase 1
  • ✅ Have Rotten Breath (dragon incantation) ready for cheese
  • ✅ Fill all flask charges
  • ✅ Equip Radagon's Soreseal or HP-boosting talismans

⚔️ Phase 1 — Gravity Warrior

🏹 Opening Arrow Barrage

▼

Radahn starts by shooting gravity arrows that track you. These one-shot most builds.

  • Method 1: Summon an NPC immediately — the arrow will target them instead.
  • Method 2: Ride Torrent and zigzag. The arrows have a slight delay — change direction constantly.
  • Method 3: Use the sword monuments on the battlefield as cover.

🐴 Mounted Combat (Torrent)

▼

This fight is designed for mounted combat. Stay on Torrent at all times.

  • Hit-and-Run: Gallop in, land 1-2 hits, gallop away. Never stay close for more than 2 seconds.
  • Double Jump: Use Torrent's double jump to avoid shockwaves and gravity slams.
  • Summon While Riding: You can summon NPCs without dismounting.
  • Healing: Create maximum distance before using flasks — his ranged attacks are fast.

⚠️ Dangerous Attacks

▼
  • Gravity Pull + Slam: Purple aura surrounds you, then you're pulled toward him and slammed. When you see the purple glow, sprint away on Torrent.
  • Sword Slam Shockwave: Slams both swords into the ground — massive AoE. Double-jump over it with Torrent.
  • Spinning Combo: Spins both swords in a whirlwind. Don't approach. Wait for the final slam.
  • Arrow Rain: Shoots a volley of arrows into the sky that rain down. Keep moving on Torrent — they target where you were, not where you are.

☄️ Phase 2 — Meteor Form

At ~50% HP, Radahn leaps into the sky and becomes a meteor. This is the most cinematic moment in the game — and the most dangerous.

🌠 The Meteor Crash

  1. When he leaps up — immediately look at the sky.
  2. You'll see a streak of fire descending. That is Radahn.
  3. Sprint perpendicular to his descent path on Torrent.
  4. The impact creates a massive explosion that one-shots most builds.
  5. After impact, he is briefly stunned — attack now.

Phase 2 New Attacks

  • Gravity Orbs: Summons 4 purple orbs that home in on you. Sprint in a wide circle on Torrent — they expire.
  • Enhanced Shockwaves: All sword slams now have larger shockwaves. Double-jump is mandatory.
  • Desperate Swings: Faster, more erratic combos. Stay at medium range and let NPCs tank.

🧀 Scarlet Rot Cheese Method

For players struggling with the fight, this method trivializes it.

Requirements

  • Rotten Breath dragon incantation (purchased from Cathedral of Dragon Communion)
  • At least 15 Faith and a Dragon Communion Seal
  • Enough FP for 1-2 casts (~36 FP)

Step-by-Step

  1. Enter the arena and immediately summon Blaidd (closest sign).
  2. While Blaidd tanks, get on Torrent and approach Radahn from the side.
  3. Cast Rotten Breath — the cloud will inflict Scarlet Rot.
  4. Once Scarlet Rot procs (he glows red), run away immediately.
  5. Scarlet Rot deals ~1,300 damage per tick to Radahn.
  6. Re-apply when the rot wears off. He will die without you ever engaging in melee.

This is the most reliable method for underleveled characters. It takes 3-4 minutes but is virtually risk-free.
