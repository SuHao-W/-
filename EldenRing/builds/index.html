⚔️ Top Meta Builds

🩸 Rivers of Blood Bleed Build PvE/PvP

Core Weapon: Rivers of Blood (Katana) + Uchigatana (off-hand)

Key Stats: ARC 45-60, DEX 40-50, VIG 40-60

  • Armor: White Mask (+10% damage when bleed procs nearby) + Lord of Blood's Exultation talisman (+20% damage on bleed)
  • Talismans: Lord of Blood's Exultation, Rotten Winged Sword Insignia, Millicent's Prosthesis, Shard of Alexander
  • Ash of War: Corpse Piler (built-in) — massive bleed buildup and ranged slashes
  • Spirit Ash: Mimic Tear +10 (duplicates your bleed output)
  • How it works: Alternate L1 attacks to build bleed rapidly. Each bleed proc triggers both White Mask and Exultation for +30% total damage.

🧙 Comet Azur Sorcery Build PvE

Core Weapon: Lusat's Glintstone Staff or Carian Regal Scepter

Key Stats: INT 70-80, MND 40-50, VIG 40

  • Spells: Comet Azur (main nuke), Terra Magica (damage buff zone), Glintstone Pebble (FP-efficient), Ranni's Dark Moon (debuffs enemy magic resistance)
  • Armor: Snow Witch Hat (+10% cold sorcery damage) or Twinsage Glintstone Crown (+6 INT, -9% HP)
  • Talismans: Graven-Mass Talisman (+8% magic damage), Magic Scorpion Charm (+12% magic damage, -10% negation), Primal Glintstone Blade (spells cost 25% less FP, -15% max HP), Godfrey Icon (boosts charged spells)
  • Flask: Cerulean Hidden Tear (zero FP consumption for 15 seconds) — combine with Comet Azur for boss melting
  • Tip: Cast Terra Magica, summon Mimic Tear to tank, then unleash Comet Azur with Cerulean Hidden Tear active.

🔨 Colossal Strength Build PvE

Core Weapon: Giant-Crusher or Ruins Greatsword

Key Stats: STR 60-80, VIG 50-60, END 30-40

  • Armor: Bull-Goat Set (highest poise in game) or Lionel's Set
  • Talismans: Great-Jar's Arsenal (+19% equip load), Green Turtle Talisman (stamina recovery), Claw Talisman (jump attacks +15%), Axe Talisman (charged attacks +10%)
  • Ash of War: Lion's Claw (hyper armor, huge damage), Royal Knight's Resolve (buffs next attack by +80%)
  • Strategy: Two-hand the weapon (1.5x STR bonus). Use jump attacks (R2 while jumping) for high poise damage. Most enemies will stagger in 2-3 hits.
  • Tip: Power-stance two Giant-Crushers for the highest per-hit damage in the game.

⚡ Faith Lightning Paladin PvE/PvP

Core Weapon: Blasphemous Blade or Winged Scythe

Key Stats: FTH 50-80, VIG 40-50, MND 25-30

  • Incantations: Lightning Spear (ranged), Flame Grant Me Strength (+20% physical/fire damage), Golden Vow (+15% all damage, +10% damage negation), Black Flame (percentage-based damage vs bosses)
  • Armor: Tree Sentinel Set or Crucible Tree Set
  • Talismans: Flock's Canvas Talisman (+8% incantation damage), Old Lord's Talisman (+30% buff duration), Two Fingers Heirloom (+5 FTH), Fire Scorpion Charm (if using Blasphemous Blade)
  • Tip: Blasphemous Blade's Ash of War (Taker's Flames) heals you for a percentage of damage dealt — incredibly sustainable.

🗡️ Dual Katana Dexterity PvE/PvP

Core Weapons: Moonveil (INT/DEX) + Uchigatana / Nagakiba

Key Stats: DEX 50-70, INT 20-40 (for Moonveil), VIG 40-50

  • Armor: Land of Reeds Set or Okina Mask (+3 DEX)
  • Talismans: Winged Sword Insignia (attack boost on successive hits), Millicent's Prosthesis (+5 DEX, boosts successive attacks), Lord of Blood's Exultation (if running bleed affinity)
  • Ash of War: Unsheathe (Uchigatana), Transient Moonlight (Moonveil built-in)
  • Strategy: Power-stance (L1) for rapid dual attacks. Moonveil's Transient Moonlight (L2+R2) deals massive poise damage and can stagger most enemies.

📐 Equip Load & Poise

Light Load (<30%)

Fastest rolls, longest iframes. Best for no-hit runs and dodge-reliant players.

Medium Load (30-70%)

Standard roll. Recommended for 90% of builds. Aim to stay just under 70%.

Heavy Load (70-100%)

Slow "fat roll." Not recommended unless using specific tank strategies with Greatshield.

Poise Breakpoints

51+ poise: resist most light attacks. 61+ poise: resist medium attacks. 101+ poise: hyper armor with heavy weapons.
