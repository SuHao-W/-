WORLD MAP & PROGRESSION

Region-by-region guide through the Lands Between. Recommended levels, key items, and boss order.

🧭 Recommended Progression Path

Limgrave → Weeping Peninsula → Liurnia → Caelid → Altus Plateau → Mt. Gelmir → Mountaintops → Farum Azula → Ashen Capital

Optional: Siofra River → Nokron → Nokstella → Deeproot Depths → Haligtree (late/endgame)

🌍 Region Details

🌿 Limgrave (Starting Area)

Recommended Level: 1-30 | Key Boss: Margit, Godrick

  • Key Items: Spirit Calling Bell (Renna at Church of Elleh, night), Torrent (Melina at Gatefront Ruins), Flask of Wondrous Physick (3rd Church of Marika)
  • Dungeons: Murkwater Cave, Coastal Cave, Highroad Cave, Fringefolk Hero's Grave
  • Notable: Limgrave is massive — explore the eastern beaches, the mistwood, and the peninsula south of the castle before challenging Stormveil.
  • Map Fragment: Found on the road east of the Gatefront Ruins Site of Grace.

🏰 Weeping Peninsula (South of Limgrave)

Recommended Level: 20-35 | Key Boss: Leonine Misbegotten

  • Key Items: 4 Sacred Tears (upgrade flasks), Golden Seeds, Stonebarb Cracked Tear
  • Notable: Often skipped but incredibly rewarding. The Church of Pilgrimage and Fourth Church hold Sacred Tears. Castle Morne has a powerful early greatsword (Grafted Blade Greatsword).
  • Tip: Complete Irina's questline here before advancing too far.

💧 Liurnia of the Lakes (West)

Recommended Level: 40-55 | Key Boss: Rennala, Queen of the Full Moon

  • Key Items: Academy Glintstone Key, Larval Tears (respec), Moonveil katana, Carian Filigreed Crest
  • Dungeons: Raya Lucaria Academy, Caria Manor, Lakeside Crystal Cave, Academy Crystal Cave
  • Notable: Three Sisters area (northwest) contains Ranni's questline — one of the most important questlines in the game. Don't skip it.
  • Map Fragments: Liurnia East, Liurnia North, Liurnia West (three separate fragments).

☠️ Caelid (East — Rot & Danger)

Recommended Level: 60-75 | Key Boss: Starscourge Radahn

  • Key Items: Radahn's Great Rune, Golden Seeds, Somber Smithing Stones, Beastman's Curved Sword
  • Notable: Extremely dangerous early. The swamp inflicts Scarlet Rot instantly. Use Preserving Boluses or Flame, Cleanse Me incantation.
  • Bestial Sanctum: Accessible early via portal in Limgrave (east of Third Church). Farm Vulgar Militia for ~1,000 runes each.
  • Redmane Castle: Festival mechanic for Radahn fight — talk to NPCs to trigger the battle.

🏔️ Altus Plateau (Central Highlands)

Recommended Level: 60-80 | Key Boss: Morgott, the Omen King

  • Key Items: Dectus Medallion (two halves in Fort Haight and Fort Faroth), Altus Plateau Map Fragment
  • Two Entry Methods: (1) Grand Lift of Dectus (requires medallion), (2) Ruin-Strewn Precipice dungeon (no medallion needed)
  • Leyndell: The Royal Capital is massive. Explore thoroughly — it contains multiple Sites of Grace, merchants, and key NPCs.
  • Notable: Windmill Village (north) has a unique boss and celebrant weapons.

🌋 Mt. Gelmir (Northwest Volcano)

Recommended Level: 70-85 | Key Boss: Rykard, Lord of Blasphemy

  • Key Items: Serpent-Hunter spear (essential for Rykard fight — found in boss arena), Gelmir's Fury sorcery
  • Volcano Manor: Joining gives you assassination contracts for unique rewards. You can complete these without hurting your main progression.
  • Notable: The mountain path is treacherous — watch for falling rocks and lava slugs.

❄️ Mountaintops of the Giants (North)

Recommended Level: 90-110 | Key Boss: Fire Giant

  • Key Items: Rold Medallion (access to Consecrated Snowfield), Giant's Forge
  • Notable: After Fire Giant, you cannot return to Leyndell in its original state — finish any quests there first.
  • Zamor Ruins: Contains Ancient Dragon Smithing Stones for +25 normal weapons.
  • Castle Sol: Required for one ending path. Contains the other half of the Haligtree Secret Medallion.

🌑 Crumbling Farum Azula (Floating City)

Recommended Level: 110-130 | Key Boss: Maliketh, the Black Blade

  • Key Items: Ancient Dragon Smithing Stones, Somber Ancient Dragon Smithing Stones
  • Notable: This area is accessed automatically after a certain point in the story. It cannot be reached by normal travel.
  • Beast Clergyman: Maliketh's first form is highly aggressive. Use pillars for cover. His second form inflicts Destined Death (max HP reduction).

🏛️ Leyndell, Ashen Capital (Endgame)

Recommended Level: 120-150 | Key Boss: Godfrey/Hoarah Loux, Radagon/Elden Beast

  • Notable: The final area. Radagon is fast and holy-focused. Elden Beast is a massive arena chase — stack Holy resistance (Haligdrake Talisman +2).
  • Tip: After defeating the final boss, you can choose to continue into NG+ or stay to explore.

📍 Map Fragment Locations

Limgrave

On the road east of Gatefront Ruins, next to a stone stele.

Liurnia

Three fragments: East (near Liurnia Highway North), North (Academy Gate Town), West (near Temple Quarter).

Caelid

On the main road south of the Smoldering Church, near a stone stele.

Altus Plateau

North of the Abandoned Coffin Site of Grace, on the road toward Leyndell.

Mountaintops

East of the Zamor Ruins, near the chain lift to the Fire Giant.

Consecrated Snowfield

Use Secret Medallion at Grand Lift of Rold. Fragment is near the first Site of Grace.
