Marcus "El Halcon" Rivera

Leader of the Lost Saints MC. A war veteran turned biker gang leader who controls the western territories.

🦅

El Halcon (The Hawk)

MEDIUM

Boss Overview

Marcus Rivera, known as "El Halcon" (The Hawk), is a former military veteran who returned from service to find his hometown controlled by criminals. He formed the Lost Saints MC as a way to take back his territory, but the gang life consumed him. Now he controls the western territories through an underground fight club and motorcycle smuggling operation.

Phase Breakdown

Phase 1: The Fight Club

+

Marcus's fight club is legendary. Getting in is your first challenge:

  • Fight Trials: Prove yourself in the underground ring
  • Climb the Ranks: Defeat multiple fighters to get Marcus's attention
  • Gain Trust: Complete jobs for the club
  • Backstage Access: Earn a meeting with Marcus

Phase 2: Club Territory

+

Taking on a biker gang means controlling the roads:

  • Highway Battles: Intense motorcycle vs vehicle combat
  • Territory Raids: Destroy gang operations in western areas
  • Armory Heist: Steal their weapons cache
  • Marcus Hunts You: He sends hit squads after you

Phase 3: Lost Saints Compound

+

The final battle at the Lost Saints headquarters:

  • Outer Perimeter: Clear the guards around the compound
  • Garage Fight: Vehicle combat in the garage
  • Biker Showdown: Marcus on his custom bike
  • Close Combat: Final confrontation in the clubhouse

Attack Patterns

  • Combat Training: Military-style hand-to-hand combat
  • Shotgun Expert: Prefers close-range firepower
  • Biker Gang: Calls in reinforcements on motorcycles
  • Vehicle Ram: Uses his truck to ram enemies
  • Territorial: Fights hardest when defending his turf
  • Reckless: Takes risks when angry
Weaknesses & Strategies
  • Anger Issues: Gets reckless when provoked
  • Predictable: Military routines make him readable
  • Hubris: Underestimates non-military opponents
  • Bikes: Strong on motorcycles, weaker on foot
  • Loyalty: His crew is loyal - take them out first
