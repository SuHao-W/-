Elena "La Vibora" Martinez

The ruthless Cartel leader whose snake-like tactics have made her one of the most feared criminals in Vice City.

🐍

La Vibora (The Snake)

HARD

Boss Overview

Elena Martinez, known as "La Vibora" (The Snake) in criminal circles, runs the most sophisticated drug operation in Vice City. Her cartel controls the southern corridors from Vice City to South America. She's intelligent, ruthless, and always three steps ahead of her enemies. Taking her down requires outsmarting a criminal mastermind.

Phase Breakdown

Phase 1: Following the Trail

+

Elena's operation is well-hidden. You must trace her network:

  • Street Dealers: Work your way up the chain from low-level dealers
  • Safe Houses: Raid multiple locations to find her distribution centers
  • Encrypted Communications: Intercept and decode messages
  • Safe House Infiltration: Discover her backup locations

Phase 2: The Drop

+

A major drug shipment is happening. This is your chance:

  • Undercover Operation: Infiltrate as new crew member
  • Shipment Location: Find where the drugs are being unloaded
  • Ambush Decision: Strike during the deal OR follow for bigger targets
  • Elena's Escape: She spots the ambush and flees

Phase 3: Snakepit

+

Elena's fortress in the swamplands:

  • Swamp Approach: Navigate through alligator-infested waters
  • Booby Traps: Disable tripwires and explosive traps
  • Inner Sanctum: Breach her fortified compound
  • Final Battle: Elena fights with her personal guard

Attack Patterns

  • Poison Darts: Uses a specialized tranquilizer dart gun
  • Environmental Traps: Leads enemies into trapped areas
  • Decoy Tactics: Uses body doubles to confuse opponents
  • Escort Contracts: Sends waves of cartel soldiers
  • Retreat Protocol: Constantly repositions during combat
  • Swamp Creatures: Uses alligators as unwilling weapons
Weaknesses & Strategies
  • Ego: Can't resist proving she's smarter than opponents
  • Hubris: Underestimates direct confrontations
  • Supply Lines: Cut off her resources before final fight
  • Swamp Navigation: Knows every path - use vehicles, not foot
  • Bodyguards: Her guards are expendable - focus on her
