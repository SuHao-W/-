Viktor "The Wolfman" Kozlov

The cold-blooded Russian Mafia boss who controls Vice City's import/export underworld.

💀

The Wolfman

EXTREME

Boss Overview

Viktor Kozlov is one of the most dangerous criminals in Vice City. Former Spetsnaz operative turned crime lord, he runs the Russian Mafia's operations with military precision. His import/export business is a front for smuggling weapons, drugs, and humans. He's methodical, patient, and extremely violent when provoked.

Phase Breakdown

Phase 1: Warehouse Raid

+

The first encounter with Viktor takes place in one of his warehouses:

  • Stealth Entry: Disable security cameras and eliminate guards silently
  • Evidence Collection: Find shipping manifests linking Viktor to illegal operations
  • Ambush: Viktor discovers you and triggers an alarm
  • Escape: Fight your way out or use vehicles in the warehouse

Phase 2: Port Infiltration

+

Viktor's shipping operation is based at the port:

  • Cargo Ship: Board a arriving cargo ship as cover
  • Security Patrol: Avoid or eliminate patrolling guards
  • Container Maze: Navigate the port's labyrinthine container yards
  • Boat Chase: Viktor escapes by boat, initiate high-speed chase

Phase 3: The Wolf's Den

+

Viktor's final base is a fortified compound:

  • Outer Perimeter: Disable surveillance and guards
  • Inner Compound: Fight through heavily armed guards
  • Armory: Viktor's personal weapons cache can be used against him
  • Direct Confrontation: Face Viktor in hand-to-hand and gun combat

Attack Patterns

  • Military Training: Uses tactical positioning and cover effectively
  • AK-74 Assualt: Prefers Russian-made weapons
  • Grenades: Throws fragmentation grenades when at range
  • Bodyguards: Elite Russian operatives with military-grade equipment
  • Wolf Pack Tactics: Coordinates with guards for flanking maneuvers
  • CQC Expert: Extremely dangerous in close quarters
Weaknesses & Strategies
  • Predictable Patrols: Military background makes him follow routines
  • Honor Bound: Won't retreat or call for backup unless necessary
  • Explosives: Vulnerable to explosives and traps
  • Arrogance: Underestimates opponents who aren't Russian
  • No Range Advantage: Prefers close combat, exploit this with distance
