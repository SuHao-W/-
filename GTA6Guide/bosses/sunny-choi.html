Sunny "Face" Choi

The Triad boss who hides behind a legitimate restaurant empire. Master of deception and deadly in combat.

🎭

Face

MEDIUM

Boss Overview

Sunny Choi earned his nickname "Face" for his ability to become whoever you need him to be. Behind his legitimate restaurant empire in the entertainment district lies the leader of a sophisticated Triad operation. He's charming, intelligent, and absolutely lethal when crossed.

Phase Breakdown

Phase 1: The Red Dragon

+

Sunny's restaurants are fronts for his operations:

  • Undercover Diner: Get a job at one of his restaurants
  • Kitchen Staff: Work your way up to inner circle
  • Secret Tunnels: Discover the passages behind the walls
  • Bookkeeping: Find records of his illegal activities

Phase 2: Nightclub Operations

+

Sunny's nightclub is where deals go down:

  • VIP Access: Get on the guest list through connections
  • Security Bypass: Navigate the tight security
  • The Back Rooms: Find where he conducts business
  • Stake Out: Watch and wait for the right moment

Phase 3: Face Off

+

The final confrontation in his private chambers:

  • Temple Infiltration: Breach his hidden triad temple
  • Honor Duel: Triad traditions require ritual combat
  • Close Quarters: Traditional weapons only
  • Final Exchange: Face-to-face confrontation

Attack Patterns

  • Karate Expert: Traditional martial arts specialist
  • Throwing Weapons: Knives and darts at range
  • Environmental: Uses the restaurant's kitchen weapons
  • Guards: Triad soldiers with martial arts training
  • Smoke Screens: Uses visual distractions to escape
  • Deception: Creates diversions and false retreats
Weaknesses & Strategies
  • Traditional: Bound by Triad honor codes
  • Overconfident: Believes in his martial arts superiority
  • Predictable: Ritualistic in combat approach
  • Guards First: His soldiers are expendable
  • Pride: Can't resist a challenge
  • Restaurants: His legitimate business is vulnerable
