Agent Chen

FBI agent investigating organized crime in Vice City. An unlikely ally or persistent enemy depending on your choices.

🎯

Federal Agent

MEDIUM

Boss Overview

Agent Chen is a dedicated FBI agent who has been tracking organized crime in Vice City for years. She's methodical, patient, and has a personal vendetta against the criminal elements that have corrupted the city. Depending on your gameplay choices, she can become either a powerful ally or a relentless adversary.

Phase Breakdown

Phase 1: The Investigation

+

Chen is always watching:

  • Surveillance Missions: She tracks your movements across the city
  • Evidence Collection: Her team gathers intel on your activities
  • Sting Operations: She sets traps to catch criminals
  • Interrogation: Can become an ally if you cooperate

Phase 2: The Chase

+

If you cross her:

  • FBI Pursuit: High-speed chases with federal backup
  • Crackdown: She targets your businesses and safe houses
  • Raid Alerts: Get ready for armed FBI teams
  • Evidence Mounting: She builds a case against you

Phase 3: Final Confrontation

+

The endgame:

  • Federal Building: Breach the FBI field office
  • Agent Combat: FBI agents provide heavy resistance
  • Chen Encounter: Face Chen in the final showdown
  • Choice Point: Take her down or turn her to your side

Attack Patterns

  • Federal Training: Expert in tactical firearms
  • Backup Calling: Can summon FBI tactical teams
  • Strategic: Plans ambushes and traps
  • Surveillance: Uses technology to track targets
  • Legal Weapon: Uses the law as a weapon
  • Persistent: Never gives up the chase
Weaknesses & Strategies
  • Rules: Bound by FBI protocols and procedures
  • Personal: Her vendetta can cloud judgment
  • Evidence: Without proof, she has no case
  • Alliance: Can be turned if you prove valuable
  • Department: She answers to superiors
