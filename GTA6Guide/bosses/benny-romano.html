Benny "The King" Romano

The old guard Italian mob boss who remembers Vice City's glory days and will do anything to maintain his power.

👑

The King

HARD

Boss Overview

Benny Romano is a relic of Vice City's criminal past. Active since the 80s, he remembers when the Italian mob ran everything. Now in his 70s but still deadly, he commands respect through decades of ruthless leadership. His connection to Vice City's history means he has contacts everywhere - from politicians to judges to old-school hitmen.

Phase Breakdown

Phase 1: Old Money

+

Benny's wealth is hidden in legitimate businesses:

  • Art Gallery: Front for money laundering operations
  • Wine Import: His legal business with illegal connections
  • Property Empire: Controls key real estate across the city
  • Old Allies: His contacts are difficult to identify

Phase 2: The Family Dinner

+

An invitation to the family compound:

  • Social Engineering: Get past his outer security
  • Witness Protection: See who else is in his network
  • Information Trading: Gather intelligence from the event
  • Unmasking: Discover who Benny truly is

Phase 3: Crown's End

+

The final confrontation at his mansion:

  • Mansion Siege: Breach his heavily fortified home
  • Past Relics: His private museum of Vice City history
  • Loyal Guards: Old-school soldiers who won't betray him
  • Final Audience: Face the King in his throne room

Attack Patterns

  • Old World Tactics: Prefers classical organized crime methods
  • Sniper: Hires top-tier assassins for long-range elimination
  • Poison: Has no problem using subtle methods
  • Family Connections: His extended family provides unlimited manpower
  • Legal Leverage: Uses lawyers and bribes to fight dirty
  • Vintage Weapons: Has access to historical weapons cache
Weaknesses & Strategies
  • Old Age: Not as physically capable as younger bosses
  • Traditional: Predictable patterns based on old methods
  • Legacy: Protecting his reputation is paramount
  • Outdated: Newer criminals have adapted beyond him
  • Legal: His legitimate businesses can be targeted
  • Hubris: Can't accept that his era is over
