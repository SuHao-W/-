Mayor Rodrigo "The Fixer"

The most powerful man in Vice City. A boss that requires cunning, patience, and precision to take down.

🎩

The Fixer

EXTREME

Boss Overview

Mayor Rodrigo is the final main story boss and represents the culmination of your criminal empire building. He controls the entire city's infrastructure through corruption, blackmail, and violence. Taking him down requires dismantling his network piece by piece before confronting him directly.

Phase Breakdown

Phase 1: The Network Infiltration

+

Before you can even touch the Mayor, you must dismantle his criminal network:

  • City Hall Missions: Infiltrate city hall as a new employee, gather evidence of corruption
  • Police Evidence: Steal evidence from Vice City PD that links the Mayor to criminal operations
  • Business Takeovers: Take over or destroy the 5 businesses that fund his operations
  • Key Witnesses: Eliminate or protect key witnesses depending on your choices

This phase tests your stealth and mission management skills. Rushing into combat will alert the Mayor's security.

Phase 2: The Press Conference

+

Once his network is weakened, the Mayor will hold an emergency press conference:

  • Stealth Approach: Position yourself in the crowd or on a nearby rooftop
  • Timing is Critical: Wait until he starts his speech before making your move
  • Two Options: Expose him publicly with evidence OR assassinate him during the chaos
  • Security Response: Immediately after exposure/attack, heavy security will converge

Choosing to expose him leads to a longer but more satisfying victory. Assassination is faster but has consequences.

Phase 3: The Final Confrontation

+

The Mayor escapes to his private yacht for the final battle:

  • Chase Sequence: Pursue his convoy through Vice City streets
  • Boat Battle: Engage his yacht with your own watercraft
  • Yacht Assault: Board the yacht and fight through elite guards
  • Duel: One-on-one confrontation with the Mayor himself

Attack Patterns

  • Bodyguards: The Mayor never fights alone. Expect 6-8 elite guards at all times
  • Counterfeit Orders: He'll call in reinforcements from various gangs (randomized)
  • Helicopter Support: During yacht phase, he calls in helicopter support
  • Escape Attempts: If you don't deal enough damage, he'll flee to another location
  • Desperation Mode: When low health, he pulls out a pistol and fights personally
Weaknesses & Strategies
  • Arrogance: He underestimates you until it's too late
  • Fixed Schedule: He follows predictable patterns during the day
  • Limited Combat Skills: He's not a fighter, he relies on others
  • Evidence: Physical evidence on his yacht damns him completely
