Terry "The Blade" OHara

The calculating Irish mob boss who runs the industrial district's underground economy.

The Blade

MEDIUM

Boss Overview

Terry "The Blade" OHara is an Irish mobster who carved out his territory in the industrial district. Known for his calculating nature and the blade tattoo that covers half his face, Terry runs a counterfeit operation that's expanded into smuggling and loan sharking. He's smarter than he looks and has survived this long by being three steps ahead.

Phase Breakdown

Phase 1: Industrial Espionage

+

Terry's operations are hidden in plain sight:

  • Factory Infiltration: His counterfeit goods are made in abandoned factories
  • Shipping Records: Track where the goods are distributed
  • Financial Trail: Follow the money to offshore accounts
  • Warehouse Raids: Destroy his storage facilities

Phase 2: The Meeting

+

Getting face-to-face with The Blade:

  • Negotiation: Pretend to be interested in business partnership
  • Inside Man: Get recruited as a new runner
  • Trust Building: Complete shipments to gain access
  • Double Cross: When you have enough info, make your move

Phase 3: Blade's Last Stand

+

The final confrontation at his headquarters:

  • Factory District: Navigate the maze of warehouses
  • Guard Elimination: Thin out his security
  • Trap Card: Use the environment against his crew
  • Final Fight: Terry wields a blade in close combat

Attack Patterns

  • Machete Combat: Close-range blade specialist
  • Environmental Aware: Uses factory hazards
  • Calculated: Plans escape routes in advance
  • Gang enforcers: Sends Irish thugs after you
  • Explosives: Uses industrial chemicals as weapons
  • Retreat Master: Knows when to fall back
Weaknesses & Strategies
  • Arrogance: Underestimates newcomers
  • Predictable: Follows patterns in his operations
  • Blades: Relies too heavily on close combat
  • Factory: His territory is vulnerable to fire
  • Loyalty: Guards are well-trained but limited in number
