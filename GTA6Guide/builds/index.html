Speculative Vehicle Categories

Based on GTA traditions and Vice City's tropical setting, here are the vehicle types we expect in GTA 6:

🏎️

Supercars

Ultra-high performance vehicles for those who need speed above all else. Expected brands include Ferrari, Lamborghini, and exclusive Vice City imports.

🚗

Muscle Cars

American classics with raw power. Perfect for street racing and intimidation. The backbone of any Vice City criminal fleet.

🏍️

Motorcycles

For those who prefer agility and the open road. Essential for evading police through tight city streets.

🚤

Boats

Vice City waters are yours to dominate. Speedboats, yachts, and fishing boats - each with unique capabilities.

✈️

Aircraft

Helicopters and planes for reconnaissance and rapid escape. The ultimate high-ground advantage.

🚛

Commercial

Semi-trucks, vans, and utility vehicles for business operations and covert transportation.
