🎯 What We Know About GTA 6

Return to Vice City

GTA 6 brings players back to Vice City, Rockstar's fictional recreation of Miami. Updated with modern graphics and an expanded map including surrounding areas beyond the city limits.

Dual Protagonists

For the first time since GTA 4, Rockstar introduces dual protagonists - Lucia and Jason, a Bonnie and Clyde-inspired criminal couple navigating the criminal underworld of Vice City.

Next-Gen Graphics

Built from the ground up for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, GTA 6 promises photorealistic graphics, real-time ray tracing, and an immersive open world experience.

Living World

The most dynamic open world ever created. NPCs with full daily routines, realistic traffic systems, evolving weather patterns, and world events that change over time.

🗺️ What to Expect in Vice City

🏖️ Beach Districts

Pristine beaches with realistic wave physics, beach goers with daily routines, water sports activities, and hidden underwater secrets. The ocean isn't just decoration - it's a playable space.

🌃 Downtown Core

Skyscrapers that actually have working elevators and accessible rooftops. Office buildings you can enter, restaurants where NPC employees have schedules, and street-level commerce that feels alive.

🏘️ Residential Areas

Suburbs with families, schools during daytime, neighborhood dynamics that shift based on player actions. Break into the wrong house and the whole community might remember.

🌴 Swamps & Rural

Beyond the city limits lies mysterious swampland, rural communities, abandoned properties, and terrain that offers both danger and opportunity for those willing to explore.
