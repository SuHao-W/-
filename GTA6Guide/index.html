🎯 What We Know About GTA 6

Return to Vice City

GTA 6 brings players back to Vice City, Rockstar's fictional recreation of Miami. Updated with modern graphics and an expanded map including surrounding areas beyond the city limits.

Dual Protagonists

For the first time since GTA 4, Rockstar introduces dual protagonists - Lucia and Jason, a Bonnie and Clyde-inspired criminal couple navigating the criminal underworld of Vice City.

Next-Gen Graphics

Built from the ground up for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, GTA 6 promises photorealistic graphics, real-time ray tracing, and an immersive open world experience.

Living World

The most dynamic open world ever created. NPCs with full daily routines, realistic traffic systems, evolving weather patterns, and world events that change over time.

🔥 Hot Theories & Predictions

The Criminal Empire Theory

Based on the trailer and Rockstar's statements, players will likely build a criminal empire from the ground up, similar to GTA: San Andreas but with much deeper progression systems. Managing businesses, territories, and crew members could be central to the gameplay loop.

The Bonnie & Clyde Narrative

The leaked footage confirms a Bonnie and Clyde dynamic between Lucia and Jason. Expect branching storylines based on player choices, with consequences for betrayals or loyalty to your partner.

Vice City's Connection to the GTA Universe

Will we see references to past GTA protagonists? Could Toni Cipriani or Ken Rosenberg make an appearance? The interconnected universe Rockstar has built suggests possible crossovers.
