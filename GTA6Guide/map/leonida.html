Leonida - Official Setting

GTA 6 takes place in the fictional state of Leonida. Last updated: 2026-06-29

✅ Confirmed

Leonida

The fictional state where GTA 6 is set. Based loosely on the state of Florida in the United States.

Vice City

The main city in Leonida. Rockstar's fictional recreation of Miami, updated for the modern era. This is the primary setting for GTA 6.

🎬 Trailer-Spotted Areas

The official trailer showed glimpses that suggest:

  • Urban environments - Modern city with skyscrapers
  • Coastal areas - Beaches and waterfront property
  • Rural/swamp areas - Beyond the main city
  • Nightlife districts - Entertainment and nightlife

❓ NOT FULLY VERIFIED: These observations from the trailer have not been officially detailed by Rockstar.
