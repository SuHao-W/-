Vice City Overview

Return to Paradise

GTA 6 returns players to Vice City, Rockstar's fictional recreation of Miami. But this isn't the Vice City of 1986 - it's a modern, sprawling metropolis with layers of history, secrets, and opportunities for criminal enterprises.

Downtown Vice City

The heart of the city. Skyscrapers, financial districts, luxury shopping, and high-stakes crime all coexist in the neon-lit streets of downtown.

Vice Beach

The famous beaches and boardwalks. Luxury condos, beach parties, tourist traps, and the dangerous underside of paradise.

Little Havana

Cultural heart of Vice City. Authentic Cuban cuisine, vibrant nightlife, and the influences of Caribbean criminal elements.

Industrial District

Warehouses, shipping yards, and manufacturing plants. Perfect for criminal operations away from prying eyes.

The Swamps

Beyond the city limits lie the mysterious swamps. Hidden compounds, wildlife, and those who prefer to stay hidden.

Vice City Keys

Island chain surrounding the main city. Exclusive properties, yacht parties, and opportunities for those who can afford the price.

Real-World Miami Comparison

Inspired by Reality

Vice City has always drawn inspiration from Miami. Based on real Miami landmarks and neighborhoods, here's what we might see:

  • South Beach: World-famous beaches and Art Deco architecture
  • Little Havana: Calle Ocho and authentic Cuban culture
  • Wynwood: Street art and trendy neighborhoods
  • Brickell: Financial district with high-rise living
  • Coral Gables: Upscale residential areas
  • Everglades: Swamplands bordering the city
  • Key Biscayne: Exclusive island paradise
