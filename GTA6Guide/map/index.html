✅ Confirmed Setting

Leonida

The fictional state where GTA 6 takes place. Based loosely on Florida, featuring a modern recreation of Miami called Vice City.

Vice City

A modern, sprawling metropolis. This is Rockstar's fictional recreation of Miami, updated for the present day.
