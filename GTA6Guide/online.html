📜 Historical Context

GTA Online History

GTA Online launched with GTA V in 2013 as a separate but integrated multiplayer mode. It evolved into a massive live service with:

  • Regular content updates
  • Microtransactions (Shark Cards)
  • Subscription service (GTA+)
  • Cross-platform play details

However, Rockstar has not confirmed if GTA 6 Online will follow the same model or feature cross-platform functionality.
