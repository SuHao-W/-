GTA 6 Characters

Meet the ambitious criminals and key players shaping the criminal underworld of Vice City.

Main Protagonists

👤

Lucia

The first female protagonist in GTA history. Lucia is a tough, street-smart criminal with a mysterious past tied to Vice City's underworld. Her relationship with Jason forms the emotional core of the story.

RoleDual Protagonist
BackgroundEx-Street Criminal
👤

Jason

Lucia's partner-in-crime, Jason is a seasoned criminal looking to make his big move in Vice City. His loyalty to Lucia will be tested as the stakes get higher and the jobs get bigger.

RoleDual Protagonist
BackgroundCareer Criminal

Anticipated Characters

Based on GTA traditions and the Vice City setting, here are the types of characters we expect to encounter:

Corrupt Politicians

Vice City's answer to Sebastian Dodd from GTA: Vice City. Politicians who run the city through corruption and will use any means necessary to maintain their power.

Drug Lords

The Cartels and local distributors who control the drug trade. Expect complex relationships and dangerous negotiations.

Gang Leaders

Various street gangs controlling different territories. Some may become allies, others will need to be eliminated.

Law Enforcement

From corrupt Vice City PD officers to federal agents. The law is both an obstacle and potential tool for your criminal empire.

Biz owners

Legitimate business owners who may have criminal connections. Nightclub owners, restaurateurs, and real estate tycoons with dark secrets.

Famous Figures

Influencers, celebrities, and socialites who inhabit Vice City's elite circles. Some missions may involve extracting information or leverage from these figures.

Possible Returns

The GTA universe is known for bringing back beloved characters across titles. Could we see:

  • Tommy Vercetti - The protagonist of GTA: Vice City, potentially as an older mentor figure or ghost from the past
  • Ken Rosenberg - The lawyer from GTA: Vice City and GTA: San Andreas who always finds himself in the criminal world
  • Pablo the Robot - Unlikely, but the GTA universe loves Easter eggs
