Boss Rewards

Defeating each boss unlocks exclusive rewards. Complete all bosses for the Ultimate Horizon Champion achievement.

🏆 Victory Crown

Complete all boss races to unlock the Victory Crown - a cosmetic item for your driver profile.

🚗 Exclusive Vehicles

Each boss drops a unique modified version of their signature car. These are not available anywhere else.

💰 Credits Bonus

Earn 5,000,000 credits per boss defeated. Complete all to earn 45,000,000 credits total.

⭐ Star Rating

Defeating all bosses grants 50 stars toward your driver rating, pushing you toward Legend status.
