Jake "Nitro" Rodriguez

Former Rally Champion - Dirt-crushing aggression with incredible recovery ability

MEDIUM

Boss Overview

Profile

Jake "Nitro" Rodriguez brought his rally skills from the dusty trails to the Horizon Festival. His aggressive driving style and ability to recover from near-crashes make him a force on any surface. He drives a rally-spec Subaru WRX STI with rocket-powered modifications.

Vehicle: Rally-spec Subaru WRX STI (700+ HP)

Specialty: Dirt tracks, rallycross, recovery driving

Weakness: Smooth tarmac, consistency on repeated laps

Phase Breakdown

Phase 1: The Aggressive Start

+

Jake launches aggressively, sometimes too aggressively. He may take unnecessary risks early to build an early lead.

Strategy:

Avoid his early chaos. Let him make mistakes while you drive clean. He often overdrives in Phase 1.

Phase 2: The Recovery Game

+

Jake's recovery ability shines. Even when pushed wide, he recovers and continues at full pace. This is his signature phase.

Strategy:

Do not rely on forcing mistakes. Instead, match his pace consistently. On tarmac sections, pull ahead as his setup favors dirt.

Phase 3: The Final Push

+

If behind, Jake takes massive risks. This is when he's most dangerous but also most vulnerable to真正的 mistakes.

Strategy:

If you have the lead, defend cleanly. If behind, his risky driving may cause a crash - wait for the opening.

🎯 Boss Weakness

Jake struggles on smooth tarmac circuits. His rally setup is optimized for rough surfaces. On clean circuit races, his advantage disappears. Additionally, his aggressive style leads to mistakes when pressured. Clean, consistent driving will often see him make errors.
