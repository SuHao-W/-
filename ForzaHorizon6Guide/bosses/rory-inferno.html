Rory "Inferno" McAllister

Hot-Headed Aussie Driver - Aggressive fire-themed builds with explosive pace

HARD

Boss Overview

Profile

Rory McAllister, known as "Inferno," is an Australian driver with a hot temper and even hotter driving style. He drives a fire-themed heavily modified car with aggressive modifications. When calm, he's extremely fast. When frustrated, he makes costly mistakes.

Vehicle: Fire-themed Modified Holden Commodore (900 HP)

Specialty: Aggressive attacks, intimidation tactics

Weakness: Temper control, clean laps under pressure

Phase Breakdown

Phase 1: The Intimidation

+

Rory tries to intimidate with aggressive early moves, including brake checking and blocking. He's testing your patience.

Strategy:

Ignore the games. Drive clean and let him build frustration. His aggression is his weakness - bait him into mistakes.

Phase 2: The Burnout

+

When his intimidation fails, Rory pushes harder. His pace becomes genuinely fast but inconsistent - occasional brilliance followed by mistakes.

Strategy:

Match his pace on good laps. If he makes a mistake, capitalize immediately. His confidence crumbles when passes are made.

Phase 3: The Rage Quit

+

If behind, Rory's temper explodes. Aggressive moves, near-crashes, and constant mistakes define this phase.

Strategy:

Survive. Do not engage with his aggression. Clean laps will see him crash or spin out eventually.

🎯 Boss Weakness

Rory is weak against patience. His hot temper causes him to make mistakes when provoked. Drive clean, avoid retaliation, and let him self-destruct. He is fastest when calm - pressure him without making contact and watch him unravel.
