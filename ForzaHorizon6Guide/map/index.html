Bonus Boards

Bonus Boards are collectible boards scattered across the map. Shoot them with the Promo Camera to earn bonus influence and unlock exclusive rewards. Total: 50 Bonus Boards.

📍 Downtown District

10 Bonus Boards scattered among skyscrapers and parking structures.

Strategy: Use the skyscraper rooftops for easy access. Look for glowing boards on building ledges.

📍 Coastal Highway

8 Bonus Boards along the beach road and pier areas.

Strategy: Drive along the beach at low speed. Many boards are at sea level on posts and signs.

📍 Mountain Region

12 Bonus Boards across winding mountain roads and scenic overlooks.

Strategy: Fastest completed during clear weather. Some boards require racing line precision to spot.

📍 Desert Plains

10 Bonus Boards in the open desert and abandoned buildings.

Strategy: Use a high-speed car. The desert is vast - speed is essential for efficiency.

📍 Forest Trails

10 Bonus Boards hidden among trees and on forest roads.

Strategy: Drive slowly and watch for boards on tree trunks and wooden structures.

Speed Traps & Speed Zones

Test your top speed at various locations across the map. Achieve the target speed to earn influence and complete challenges.

🏁 Highway Speed Trap

Location: Coastal Highway - Long Straight

Target: 180 MPH

Best Vehicle: S-Class top speed builds

🏔️ Mountain Speed Zone

Location: West Mountain Pass

Target: 150 MPH

Strategy: Use downhill momentum for extra speed

🏜️ Desert Speed Trap

Location: Salt Flats - Open Desert

Target: 200 MPH

Strategy: Use a straight-line speed demon build

🌲 Forest Speed Zone

Location: Main Forest Road

Target: 120 MPH

Strategy: Tight corners require handling builds

Danger Signs

Leap across marked gaps and signs to earn influence. Some require specific vehicles or tuning setups.

🚧 Canyon Leap

Location: Main Canyon Road

Distance: 450 feet

Tip: Use a high-speed car with good aerodynamics

🌉 Bridge Jump

Location: Coastal Bridge

Distance: 350 feet

Tip: Approach at 45° angle for maximum distance

🏔️ Mountain Jump

Location: Summit Road

Distance: 500 feet

Tip: Use a vehicle with rally suspension for landing
