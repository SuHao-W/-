Characters & Drivers

Meet the personalities that make the Horizon Festival legendary. From rival racers to key figures in the racing scene.

These skilled drivers will challenge you throughout the Horizon Festival. Each has unique strengths, driving styles, and story backgrounds.

Vehicle: Modified Toyota Supra A90

Difficulty: EXTREME

Former Formula racer who transitioned to street racing after her F1 career ended controversially. Her precision on straights is unmatched, often leaving opponents in her dust.

Festival Organizers

Festival Director - Alexandra Mills

+

The mastermind behind the Horizon Festival. Alexandra is a former motorsport executive who left her corporate career to create the ultimate celebration of car culture. She's hands-on with every event and genuinely cares about the drivers.

Role: Main quest giver, festival expansion

Location: Horizon Festival HQ

Head Mechanic - "Big Mike" Thompson

+

A retired NASCAR crew chief with 30 years of experience. Big Mike runs the garage and will help you tune your vehicles. He's gruff but fair, and his mechanical knowledge is unmatched.

Role: Vehicle upgrades, tuning assistance

Location: Horizon Garage

PR Manager - Derek Song

+

The voice of the Horizon Festival. Derek handles all communications, sponsor relations, and media events. Always has the latest gossip about rival racers.

Role: Sponsors, PR events, tips about other racers

Location: Media Center

Horizon Tour Guide - Isabella "Izzy" Costa

+

A local who knows every hidden spot in the map. Izzy leads expedition events and reveals secret locations. She's your go-to person for barn find locations and hidden jumps.

Role: Expeditions, hidden locations, collectibles

Location: Various points around the map

Driver Attributes

As you progress through the game, you'll earn Driver Tokens to improve your attributes. Each attribute affects different aspects of gameplay.

🏎️ Speed

Affects top speed and acceleration in straight lines. Essential for drag races and highway events. Max level unlocks the Speed Trap challenges.

🌀 Handling

Improves cornering ability and car control. Critical for technical circuits and street racing. Higher handling reduces understeer and oversteer issues.

💨 Drift

Unlocks drift-specific events and improves drift score multipliers. Essential for the drift zone challenges and drift story missions.

🔥 Off-Road

Improves performance on dirt, mud, and rough terrain. Required for rally events and certain barn find access roads. Makes off-road racing significantly easier.

⭐ Star Rating

Your overall driver rating. Earned by completing events, challenges, and collecting items. Higher star ratings unlock new event tiers and exclusive races.

🏆 Influence

Currency used to unlock new events, influence festival sites, and progress the main story. Earned through racing, discovering locations, and completing challenges.
