Prosthetic tool loadouts, skill combinations, and combat art setups for every playstyle.

⚔️ Top Meta Loadouts

🔥 Firecracker + Flame Vent (Aggressive) PvE

Slot 1: Shinobi Firecracker | Slot 2: Flame Vent | Slot 3: Loaded Axe

  • Strategy: Firecracker stuns, Flame Vent deals fire damage, Axe breaks shields.
  • Best Against: Most bosses, shielded enemies, red-eyed enemies (weak to fire).
  • Upgrades: Spring-load Firecracker (faster), Okinaga's Flame Vent (wider), Sparking Axe (stun).
  • Combat Art: Whirlwind Slash or Ichimonji Double.
  • Tip: Firecrackers work on almost every boss. Keep them equipped at all times.

🛡️ Umbrella + Mist Raven (Defensive) Survival

Slot 1: Loaded Umbrella | Slot 2: Mist Raven | Slot 3: Shuriken

  • Strategy: Umbrella blocks unblockables, Mist Raven teleports through attacks, Shuriken for range.
  • Best Against: Terror enemies, Headless, Shichimen Warriors, Isshin Phase 3.
  • Upgrades: Magnet Umbrella (projectile deflection), Aged Feather Mist Raven (follow-up attack).
  • Combat Art: High Monk (counters sweeps) or Ashina Cross (quick damage).
  • Tip: The Umbrella's "Projected Force" follow-up deals massive damage after blocking.

⚡ Shuriken + Chasing Slice (Speed) Mobility

Slot 1: Shuriken | Slot 2: Sabimaru | Slot 3: Finger Whistle

  • Strategy: Shuriken + Chasing Slice closes distance instantly. Sabimaru poisons. Whistle stuns beasts.
  • Best Against: Fast enemies, jumping enemies, groups of weak mobs.
  • Upgrades: Gouging Top (multi-hit shuriken), Piercing Sabimaru (faster poison).
  • Combat Art: Spiral Cloud Passage or Floating Passage.
  • Tip: Chasing Slice after shuriken is one of the best gap-closers in the game.

🎯 Spear + Divine Abduction (Utility) Farming

Slot 1: Loaded Spear | Slot 2: Divine Abduction | Slot 3: Firecracker

  • Strategy: Spear for pulling and range. Abduction for instant kills on weak enemies. Firecracker for stuns.
  • Best Against: Mini-bosses, farming routes, Guardian Ape (spear trick).
  • Upgrades: Corkscrew Spear (centipede pull), Golden Vortex (more sen from abduction).
  • Combat Art: Mortal Draw (high damage) or Empowered Mortal Draw.
  • Tip: Golden Vortex + Mibu Balloon of Wealth = massive sen farming.

🎯 Prosthetic Upgrade Priority

Early Game Priority

  1. Spring-load Firecracker — faster activation
  2. Loaded Shuriken — more damage
  3. Spring-load Axe — faster swing

Mid Game Priority

  1. Magnet Umbrella — projectile deflection
  2. Okinaga's Flame Vent — wider burn area
  3. Cleaving Spear — pull + sweep attack

Late Game Priority

  1. Lazulite Shuriken — piercing + spirit damage
  2. Suzaku Umbrella — fire + terror immunity
  3. Lazulite Sacred Flame — max fire damage

Materials

Scrap Iron: Early upgrades. Scrap Magnetite: Mid upgrades. Lapis Lazuli: Final upgrades (only 6 per playthrough, choose wisely).
