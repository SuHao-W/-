SEKIRO: SHADOWS DIE TWICE GUIDE

Your ultimate shinobi companion. Boss strategies, prosthetic builds, combat techniques, and region-by-region walkthroughs for the One-Armed Wolf.

📋 What Players Need

🎯 Master Deflection

Sekiro's core is the "clash of steel." Players need to understand deflection timing, posture mechanics, and how to counter Perilous Attacks (Mikiri for thrusts, jump for sweeps, dodge for grabs).

⚔️ Boss Strategies

Every boss has unique attack patterns. Players need phase breakdowns, move counters, and prosthetic setups to create damage windows.

🦾 Prosthetic Builds

10 prosthetic tools each serve a purpose: firecrackers for stuns, shuriken for chase, umbrella for defense, Sabimaru for poison. Smart loadouts dramatically lower difficulty.

🗺️ Exploration Rewards

Prayer Beads (boost vitality/posture), Gourd Seeds (extra heals), and Treasure Carp Scales (trade items) are hidden across the map. Missing them directly impacts difficulty.

💀 Death & Resurrection

Dragonrot, resurrective power, and Unseen Aid. Players need to understand death penalties and how to manage revive charges during critical fights.

🎭 Multiple Endings

4 endings (Immortal Severance, Purification, Return, Shura). Certain bosses and areas are tied to ending choices — easy to miss without guidance.
