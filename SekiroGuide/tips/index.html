TIPS & TECHNIQUES

Combat fundamentals, stealth mastery, and essential knowledge every shinobi needs.

🎮 Combat Fundamentals

Deflection Timing

Press L1/Block just before the attack lands. The timing window is generous — about 0.3 seconds. You'll hear a loud "CLANG" and see sparks when successful.

  • Deflecting deals posture damage to the enemy
  • Deflecting recovers your own posture slightly
  • Most boss combos end with a deflectable final hit — learn the rhythm

Perilous Attacks (危)

Red kanji means unblockable. Three types, three counters:

  • Thrust (突): Dodge forward → Mikiri Counter (massive posture damage)
  • Sweep (下): Jump and press R1 → Jump Kick (posture damage)
  • Grab (掴): Run away — cannot be countered

Posture System

Both you and enemies have posture meters. When full = stunned and vulnerable to deathblow.

  • Posture recovers when holding guard
  • Enemy posture recovers slower when their HP is low
  • Deathblow enemies to permanently kill them (most mini-bosses)

Stealth & Deathblows

Sneak behind enemies for instant kills. Essential for mini-bosses with multiple health bars.

  • Crouch (L3) to move silently
  • Use walls and grass for cover
  • Airborne Deathblow from above
  • Wall hug and peek for stealth kills

Healing Management

Healing Gourd has limited charges. Find Gourd Seeds to upgrade.

  • 9 Gourd Seeds total = 10 charges max
  • Pellets heal slowly over time — use before fights
  • Rice and Divine Grass are emergency heals
  • Healing during boss fights creates openings — use firecrackers first

Dragonrot & Resurrection

Dying and using Resurrection spreads Dragonrot to NPCs. They become sick and cannot interact.

  • Use Dragon's Blood Droplet to cure all NPCs at once
  • Droplets are limited — use sparingly
  • True Death (no resurrection) does NOT spread Dragonrot
  • Dragonrot reduces your Unseen Aid (free revive chance)

🗺️ Exploration & Progression

  1. Talk to Sculptor often — he unlocks new prosthetic upgrades and hints
  2. Check merchant inventories — they sell key items, prosthetic materials, and Gourd Seeds
  3. Rest at Sculptor's Idols — respawns enemies but saves progress
  4. Buy Coin Purses — store sen safely. Death makes you lose 50% of carried sen
  5. Use Mibu Balloons — increase item drop rate, sen gain, or XP gain for 150 seconds
  6. Hirata Estate is optional but essential — contains 2 Prayer Beads and the Flame Vent
  7. Sunken Valley and Senpou Temple can be done in either order
  8. Don't skip mini-bosses — they drop Prayer Beads (vitality upgrades)
  9. Headless fights are optional — but drop Spiritfall candies (infinite buffs)
  10. Check for hidden paths — many walls can be broken, ceilings can be grappled

⚡ Pro Tips (Spoiler-Free)

Farming XP Late Game

Best spot: Ashina Outskirts (after castle invasion). Antechamber Idol → kill 5 enemies → rest → repeat. ~3,000 XP per minute.

Farming Sen

Best spot: Fountainhead Palace. Use Golden Vortex prosthetic + Mibu Balloon of Wealth. Each enemy drops 2-3x sen.

Skill Point Farming

Endgame Ashina Castle (burning). Run from Idol to Idol killing everything. With Bell Demon, ~5,000+ XP per run.

All Prayer Beads

There are 40 Prayer Beads total (10 vitality upgrades). 4 from main bosses, 36 from mini-bosses and hidden locations. Missable ones are in Hirata Estate (second visit) and Fountainhead Palace.

Gourd Seeds

9 seeds total = 10 gourd charges. Check every merchant, especially Memorial Mob vendors. One is hidden in Ashina Castle pond.

Lapis Lazuli

Only 6 per playthrough. Needed for final prosthetic upgrades. Sources: Fountainhead Palace (2), Demon of Hatred (1), Shura ending boss (2), carp quest (1).
