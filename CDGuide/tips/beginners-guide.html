🚀 Getting Started

🎯 First Steps

When you start the game, take your time to:

  • Watch the intro cutscene to understand the story
  • Complete the tutorial thoroughly - don't skip!
  • Get comfortable with the controls
  • Explore the starting area completely
⏱️ Take Your Time

There's no rush! Crimson Desert is meant to be explored slowly. Enjoy the journey!

🎮 Basic Controls

Movement

  • WASD / Left Stick - Move your character
  • Space / A Button - Jump / Dodge
  • Shift / B Button - Sprint
  • C / Right Stick Click - Crouch

Combat

  • Left Click / RT - Light Attack
  • Right Click / LT - Heavy Attack
  • L1 / LB - Block / Parry
  • R1 / RB - Special Ability
  • 1-4 / D-Pad - Quick Items

⚔️ Combat Basics

🎯 Attacking 101

Light Attacks: Fast, low damage. Good for combos and quick hits.

Heavy Attacks: Slow, high damage. Breaks enemy guards.

Combo Tips:

  • Light → Light → Heavy = Knockback combo
  • Heavy → Light → Light = Stun combo
  • Hold Heavy for charged attack
🛡️ Defending & Dodging

Blocking: Hold block button to reduce damage. Watch your stamina!

Perfect Parry: Block right before impact to stun enemies. Timing is key!

Dodging:

  • Dodge into attacks can avoid damage completely
  • Perfect dodge (last second) grants immunity
  • Dodge multiple times can create distance
💪 Stamina Management
⚠️ Critical Tip

Never let your stamina hit zero!

  • Stamina regenerates when not attacking/dodging
  • Each dodge uses ~25% stamina
  • Heavy attacks use more stamina
  • Walk to recover stamina faster

🗺️ Exploration Guide

🔍 What to Look For
  • Chests: Contain loot, gear, and resources
  • NPCs: Give quests and valuable info
  • Fast Travel Points: Unlock these ASAP!
  • Secrets: Hidden paths, caves, and treasures
  • Resources: Herbs, ores, crafting materials
📍 Fast Travel System

How to Unlock: Approach and activate a fast travel point (usually a stone monument).

How to Use: Open map, select a point you've unlocked, and confirm travel.

Pro Tip: Unlock every point you find - it makes backtracking much easier!

🌿 Collecting Resources
  • Herbs: Used for crafting potions and medicine
  • Ores: Needed for weapons and armor upgrades
  • Leather: From defeated animals, used for gear
  • Rare Items: Look for glowing plants and crystals
💡 Pro Tip

Collect everything - you'll be glad you did later!

🎯 How to Get XP
  • Main Quests: Biggest XP reward - always prioritize!
  • Side Quests: Great XP and usually fun stories
  • Enemies: Kill enemies for XP (scale with level)
  • Bosses: Huge XP payout - but challenging!
  • Exploration: Discover new areas for bonus XP
⭐ Skill Points

Each level gives you skill points to spend:

  • Combat Skills: New attacks and combos
  • Defense Skills: Better blocking and dodging
  • Utility Skills: Quality of life improvements
💡 Recommendation

Unlock 1-2 combat skills first, then invest in defense!

⚔️ Gear & Equipment

🗡️ Weapons

  • Sword: Balanced, all-around choice
  • Axe: Heavy damage, slow speed
  • Spear: Long reach, quick attacks
  • Bow: Ranged damage option

🛡️ Armor

  • Light: More mobility, less defense
  • Medium: Balance between defense and mobility
  • Heavy: Great defense, less mobility
🎒 Item Management
  • Always carry healing items (at least 10)
  • Keep buff potions for boss fights
  • Sell duplicate items to merchants
  • Upgrade your gear whenever possible

🌱 Early Game Tips (First 5 Hours)

✅ Things to Do First

  1. Complete the entire tutorial
  2. Explore the starting village thoroughly
  3. Talk to every NPC (they give quests!)
  4. Unlock all nearby fast travel points
  5. Gather resources as you explore
  6. Complete at least 3 side quests
  7. Buy your first weapon upgrade
  8. Save before every major fight

🎒 What to Collect

  • Every herb you see (green plants)
  • All chests in each area
  • Any rare glowing items
  • Money from enemies and quests

❌ Common Mistakes to Avoid

1️⃣ Don't Rush!

It's not a race. Explore, talk to NPCs, and enjoy the game!

2️⃣ Don't Ignore Side Quests

They give great rewards and help you level up!

3️⃣ Don't Forget to Save

Save often - especially before boss fights and dangerous areas!

4️⃣ Don't Neglect Upgrades

Upgrade your gear regularly - it makes a huge difference!

5️⃣ Don't Use All Items At Once

Save some healing items and buffs for boss fights!

🏆 Final Tips for Success

✅ DO

  • Learn from your mistakes
  • Experiment with different weapons
  • Explore every corner
  • Talk to everyone
  • Have fun!

❌ DON'T

  • Get frustrated - you'll get better!
  • Compare yourself to others
  • Skip cutscenes (they're good!)
  • Forget to rest/save
  • Give up too easily
