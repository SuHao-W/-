❓ FAQ

Frequently asked questions and answers

🎮 What is Crimson Desert?

Crimson Desert is an open-world action RPG developed by Pearl Abyss. Set in a vast, living world, it follows the journey of warriors fighting for survival and freedom.

💻 What platforms is it available on?

Crimson Desert is available on PC, PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X/S.

🧙 Can I change characters?

Yes, you can switch between characters at specific points in the game or at your home base.

⚔️ Is there multiplayer?

Crimson Desert features both single-player and cooperative multiplayer modes.

⏱️ How long is the game?

The main story takes approximately 30-40 hours, with additional side content and exploration adding many more hours.

📦 Is there DLC?

Yes, there are plans for post-launch DLC including new story content, characters, and regions.

🎯 What's the best starting character?

The Knight or Warrior are great choices for beginners due to their straightforward playstyles and forgiving mechanics.

🔧 Can I respec my character?

Yes, you can respec your character's skills and abilities at certain locations in the game.
