🔥 Fire Elemental

A pure manifestation of flame and fury

Overview

The Fire Elemental is fast and aggressive. Constant fire damage and explosions make this a chaotic fight.

Medium
Difficulty
❄️
Ice Weakness
🔥
Fire Immunity

Phase Breakdown

Phase 1 (100% - 50% HP)

Dash attacks and fireballs.

  • Fire Dash: Dodge perpendicular
  • Fireball: Side step

Phase 2 (50% - 0% HP)

Split into smaller elementals.

  • Split: Clear small ones first
  • Explosion: Keep distance

Recommended Build

🔮 Mage - Ice Build

Ice damage is super effective. Freeze effects slow it down.
