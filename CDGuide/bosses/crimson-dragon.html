🐉 Crimson Dragon

The fearsome guardian of the ancient temple

Overview

The Crimson Dragon is one of the most challenging bosses in the game. This ancient guardian protects the temple's secrets with devastating fire attacks and powerful physical strikes.

Very Hard
Difficulty
🔥
Fire Weakness
❄️
Ice Resistance

Phase Breakdown

Phase 1 (100% - 70% HP)

The dragon starts with basic fire attacks and tail swipes. Focus on learning patterns.

  • Fire Breath: Dodge to the side
  • Tail Swipe: Roll backward
  • Claw Strike: Block or dodge

Phase 2 (70% - 40% HP)

The dragon becomes more aggressive, adding flight and firebombs.

  • Flight: Keep moving to avoid swoops
  • Firebomb: Spread out if playing multiplayer
  • Rage: Increased attack speed

Phase 3 (40% - 0% HP)

The final phase with devastating area attacks.

  • Meteor Shower: Find safe spots
  • Fire Nova: Get behind cover
  • Berserk: All attacks amplified

Recommended Build

🏹 Archer - Sniper Build

Stay at range and focus on weak points. Use ice attacks to exploit vulnerability.

  • Ice Arrows for damage bonus
  • Precision Shot for weak point damage
  • Eagle Eye for crit chance

Tips & Tricks

🎯 Aim for Weak Points

The dragon's head and chest are weak points - focus your attacks there for massive damage.

⚠️ Watch for Stagger

When the dragon staggers, use the opportunity to unleash your most powerful attacks.
