❄️ Ice Giant

A colossal being frozen in time

Overview

The Ice Giant is slow but incredibly powerful. Freezing attacks and ice storms require careful positioning.

Hard
Difficulty
🔥
Fire Weakness
❄️
Ice Immunity

Phase Breakdown

Phase 1 (100% - 60% HP)

Ice fists and freeze breath.

  • Freeze Breath: Dodge behind
  • Ice Fist: Roll away

Phase 2 (60% - 0% HP)

Ice storms and ground spikes.

  • Ice Storm: Keep moving
  • Spikes: Watch ground patterns

Recommended Build

🔮 Mage - Fire Build

Fire damage melts through ice armor. Focus on high AoE damage.
