🐊 Swamp Lord

A monstrous creature ruling the murky swamps

Overview

The Swamp Lord is a slow but powerful boss. Poison attacks and environmental hazards make this fight tricky.

Medium
Difficulty
🔥
Fire Weakness
☠️
Poison Immunity

Phase Breakdown

Phase 1 (100% - 50% HP)

Basic attacks and poison pools.

  • Poison Pool: Avoid standing in green areas
  • Body Slam: Roll sideways

Phase 2 (50% - 0% HP)

Summons minions and toxic rain.

  • Minions: Clear them first
  • Toxic Rain: Find cover

Recommended Build

⚔️ Warrior - Berserker

High burst damage to quickly take down the boss. Use fire enchants.
