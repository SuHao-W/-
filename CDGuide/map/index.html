Region guides and collectible locations

World Regions

🏰 Starting Region

The beginning of your journey, home to the tutorial and early quests.

  • ✅ 5 Treasure Chests
  • ✅ 3 Side Quests
  • ✅ 2 Fast Travel Points
  • ✅ 1 Boss Encounter

🌲 Forest Region

A dense forest teeming with wildlife and hidden secrets.

  • ✅ 8 Treasure Chests
  • ✅ 5 Side Quests
  • ✅ 4 Fast Travel Points
  • ✅ 2 Boss Encounters

🏜️ Desert Region

Harsh desert environment with ancient ruins and powerful enemies.

  • ✅ 10 Treasure Chests
  • ✅ 7 Side Quests
  • ✅ 5 Fast Travel Points
  • ✅ 3 Boss Encounters

❄️ Mountain Region

Frozen peaks with treacherous paths and valuable rewards.

  • ✅ 12 Treasure Chests
  • ✅ 8 Side Quests
  • ✅ 6 Fast Travel Points
  • ✅ 3 Boss Encounters

Collectibles Guide

💎 Treasure Chests

Look for glowing chests in every region - they contain valuable loot, gear, and resources.

  • Check hidden corners and caves
  • Complete puzzles to unlock chests
  • Some chests require specific keys
📜 Lore Items

Collect lore items to uncover the world's history and secrets.

  • Found in buildings and ruins
  • Often hidden behind objects
  • Unlocks additional story content
🗿 Ancient Relics

Rare and powerful items with unique effects.

  • Very rare spawns
  • Often guarded by tough enemies
  • Provides unique bonuses
